## 中文摘要

航天器由于故障、完全失效或任务结束而被放弃后，停留在空间将成为太空垃圾，不但占用了宝贵的轨道资源，还可能危及其它航天器的安全，对故障卫星、失效航天器及轨道垃圾等空间非合作目标的在轨服务需求变得越来越迫切。对空间非合作目标进行接近观测和3D建模是实现在轨服务的关键，而非合作目标的高动态性、大实体性和空间环境剧烈变化成为制约接近观测和3D建模发展的重要瓶颈。本项目以空间非合作目标的接近观测与3D建模为背景，重点围绕空间非合作目标的快速避障和接近观测多任务协同三维运动规划、局部观测的激光和图像多点云融合高效3D建模和非合作目标的绕飞观测与3D建模全流程闭环演示三方面开展深入研究，突破传统运动规划和3D建模方法性能受目标特性和空间环境剧烈变化的限制，为空间非合作目标在轨接近观测和3D建模的实现提供基础理论和关键技术支撑。

## 英文摘要

After the spacecraft is abandoned due to malfunction, complete failure or the end of mission, staying in the space will become space debris, which not only occupies valuable orbital resources, but also may endanger the safety of other spacecraft. The demand for on-orbit service of non-cooperative targets in the space such as failure satellites, invalid spacecraft and orbital garbage is becoming more and more urgent.  Proximity inspection and 3D modeling operations for non-cooperative target are the key to achieving on-orbit service. However, the development of proximity inspection and 3D modeling for non-cooperative target is restricted by some bottleneck problems, such as the target's high dynamics and large solidity and dramatic changes in the space environment. This project aims at proximity inspection and 3D modeling for non-cooperative targets in the space. Three-dimensional motion planning in consideration of multi-task coordination between rapid obstacle avoidance and proximity inspection of non-cooperative targets and local inspection efficient 3D modeling by fusing laser and image point cloud data are discussed and applied in spacecraft proximity inspection and 3D modeling missions in the form of full-process closed-loop demonstration. Our research can overcome the performance limitation of traditional related proximity inspection and 3D modeling methods caused by the properties of non-cooperative target and dramatic changes in the space environment, and further supply key theoretical and technical support for the realization of on-orbit proximity inspection and 3D modeling operations for non-cooperative target in the space.
