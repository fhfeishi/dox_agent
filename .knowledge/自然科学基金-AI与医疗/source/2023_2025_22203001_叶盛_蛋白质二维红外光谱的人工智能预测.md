## 中文摘要

蛋白质分子动态过程中的结构探测是理解其生命功能的关键，也是现代科学重要的前沿问题之一。发展能够实时探测蛋白质动态结构的二维红外光谱技术是当前一个重要的科学机遇。然而，目前还很难对采集获得的实验光谱进行快速的解读并将谱学特征变化归属于结构的特定演化。事实上，由于蛋白质结构的复杂度极高，其二维红外光谱的理论模拟面临着严重的计算瓶颈问题，限制了实验光谱的理论解读，也严重阻碍了二维红外光谱实时探测技术的实际应用。.本项目拟采用数据驱动的人工智能方法，结合量子化学计算、分子动力学模拟，突破传统蛋白质二维红外光谱模拟的计算化学瓶颈，发展基于量子力学精度的蛋白质二维红外光谱的人工智能预测方案，建立蛋白质结构变化与谱学响应之间的构效关系，为蛋白质分子动态结构的二维红外光谱研究提供有力支撑和理论指导。

## 英文摘要

The dynamic structure determination of protein is the key to understand its functions, which is also one of the important issues in modern science. Two-dimensional infrared spectroscopy techniques provide powerful toolkits to decipher proteins dynamic structures. However, the theoretical interpretation of spectroscopic signals and connecting them with structural detail is an expensive task, which requires many electronic structure calculations at the quantum chemistry level for a large number (typically thousands) of representative configurations in a fluctuating environment..    This project will employ data-driven artificial intelligence techniques combined with quantum chemical calculation and molecular dynamics simulation, to address the computational chemistry challenge of two-dimensional infrared spectra simulation of proteins. We aim to present a novel machine learning protocol that uses a few key structural descriptors to rapidly predict the two-dimensional infrared spectra of various proteins by establishing the structure-property relationship from a large database of molecular structures and spectra signals. We believe that our project will strongly support studies of spectroscopy with protein dynamics in life science.
