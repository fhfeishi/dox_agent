## 中文摘要

设计康复机器人变阻抗控制，实现多康复模式下的人机协作，可有效提高康复的安全与舒适。然而，已有患者运动意图估计精度的不足和康复模式的切换严重影响多康复模式下人机协作：运动意图估计精度不足影响主动康复；面向康复模式切换的期望变阻抗动态是一个切换线性时变系统，目前无变阻抗参数规划方法来保证该动态稳定；已有阻抗控制是在被动或主动康复模式下设计，康复模式切换会导致控制的不连续性易引起抖振问题，影响控制的精度且易造成系统振荡或失稳。为此，本项目提出面向多康复模式下人机协作的康复机器人变阻抗控制：(1)基于卡尔曼滤波和神经网络，提出运动意图的多源信息融合估计，实现对患者运动意图的精确估计；(2)优化期望变阻抗参数，保证期望切换变阻抗动态的稳定；(3)提出自适应模型预测变阻抗控制，利用滚动优化协调提高控制的光滑性和控制性能,实现多康复模式下人机协作。本研究将为提高机器人辅助康复的安全和舒适作出贡献。

## 英文摘要

Designing variable impedance control for rehabilitation robots to realize human-robot cooperation in multiple rehabilitation modes can improve safety and comfortability of the robot-assisted rehabilitation. However, the deficiency in the estimation accuracy of patient’s desired motions and switches of rehabilitation modes affect the human-robot cooperation in multiple rehabilitation modes: The deficiency in the estimation accuracy of patient’s desired motions affects active rehabilitation；The desired variable impedance dynamics in the switches of rehabilitation modes is a switched linear time-varying system and now no existing programming method for variable impedance profiles can be used to guarantee the stability of the desired impedance dynamics; Since the existing impedance controllers were designed in each rehabilitation mode, the switches of rehabilitation modes will make the controllers be discontinuous and possibly result in the chattering problem which affects control accuracy and possibly result in oscillation or instability of the control systems. Therefore, this project proposes variable impedance control of rehabilitation robots for human-robot cooperation in multiple rehabilitation modes. The study mainly includes: (1) Based on Kalman-filter and neural networks, proposing a multiple-source information fusion method to estimate patient’s desired motions accurately; (2) Optimizing the desired variable impedance profiles to guarantee the stability of the desired switched variable impedance dynamics; (3) Proposing a model predictive-based adaptive variable impedance controller to achieve human-robot cooperation in multiple rehabilitation modes, where control smoothness and control performances are cooperatively improved through a receding horizon optimization. This study will make contributions to improve the safety and comfortability of robot-assisted rehabilitation.

## 结题摘要

本项目旨在提高机器人辅助康复的安全性和舒适性，提出了面向多康复模式下人机协作的上肢康复机器人变阻抗控制策略，包括：(1)提出了基于高斯过程的多源信息融合意图预测以及基于时域卷积网络的信息融合意图预测方法，对生机电信息进行了多层次、多空间信息互补，提高了连续运动意图预测精度；(2)建立了时间域（空间域）阻抗参数学习策略，提高了人机阻抗匹配性；(3)设计了多康复模式下人机交互控制，保证了各康复模式下任务的完成以及康复模式间的无缝切换。项目执行期间，发表SCI检索论文7篇、EI检索论文6篇，申请发明专利 3项。(协助)培养博士研究生2名，培养硕士研究生5名。圆满完成了项目预期指标。本项目的研究促进了康复机器人理论的发展，并为其应用提供了重要技术支持。
