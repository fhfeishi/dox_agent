## 项目摘要

## 中文摘要:

从物理机理上深刻理解和精准构筑描述单原子催化剂（SACs）稳定性和催化效率的主导“描述符”，并以此为依据提升SACs的稳定性和优化其新奇物理化学特性是当今凝聚态物理、化学、材料等交叉学科中发展和制备经济、高效、功能型催化剂材料和器件的重点研究课题之一。本项目拟采用基于第一性原理为主的多尺度模拟计算与机器学习研究方法相结合，以CO的催化氧化为例，创新性地提出:通过厘清锚定单原子活性位点的载体（Substrate）的特征描述符S（比如功函数和还原性等）与已知的金属单原子（Atom）的内秉属性描述符A（比如电负性等）以及金属单原子与载体相互耦合（Cou pling）的描述符C（比如配位数和结合能等）之间的关联关系，从而构筑出描述SACs稳定性和催化效率的主导描述符D与S-A-C之间的明确对应关系，最终建立起SACs稳定性和催化性能的精准调控机理，为实验上制备新型高效的SACs提供重要的理论借鉴。

## Abstract:

Constructing the dominant "descriptor" to describe the stability and catalytic efficiency of single-atom catalysts (SACs) from the physical mechanism point of view, enhancing their stabilities and optimizing their exotic physical and chemical properties are of the most fundamental and important research topics in the fields of condensed matter of physics, chemistry, and material science in establishing economic, highly efficient, and functional materials and devices. Here, taking CO oxidation as a prototypical example, we adopt multi-scale computational simulations combined with machine learning to systematically investigate and specifically establish the dominated descriptor (D) of the stability and reactivity of SACs. Specifically, we perform in-depth analysis on the relationship of the well-established descriptors of the supporting substrate (S), such as work function and reducibility, free-standing single atoms (A), such as electronegativity, and the descriptor that accounts for the coupling (C) of the deposited single atoms and the substrate, such as coordination number and binding energy, to unambiguously establish the new dominant descriptor D of the SACs systems in reflecting the stability and reactivity of SACs stabilized on the substrate, via figuring out the specific relationship of the D and S-A-C. Based on this, we establish effective modulation mechanism to achieve highly stable and excellently catalytic SACs. The present proposal is expected to provide valuable theoretical guidelines in developing new highly efficient SACs.

## Abstract (Brief description of research background, main methods, contributions, and research data):

This project based on first-principles density functional theory, and systematically investigates the interaction mechanisms between supports and transition atoms, constructs dominant “descriptors" for describing the stability and catalytic efficiency of SACs, and establishes precise regulation mechanisms for SAC catalytic performance.

The main findings are as follows: (a)Alkali-metal-anchored SACs on defect-free Ti02(110) : Using Li, Na, and K atoms, self-assembled stabilization of Pd/Pt single atoms is achieved on surfaces, forming an AMA2-0-Pd/Pt structure with significantly enhanced CO oxidation activity. A strong correlation between the valence state of Pt/Pd and catalytic activity is revealed, establishing it as a descriptor for catalytic activity. (JACS, submitted). (b)P-doped PtCo nanoflowers for seawater electrolysis hydrogen production: P doping shifts the d-band center of Pt downward by 0.038 eV, optimizing hydrogen adsorption energy. In natural seawater, an overpotential of only 18.2 mV is required to achieve 10 mA cm-2, with stable operation for 500 hours. This offers a new electronic structure regulation strategy for seawater electrolysis catalysts (ACS AMI, submitted). (c)2D multiferroic materials for room-temperature CO catalysis: The Cr(h-fpyz)2 single-atom catalyst exhibits a reaction barrier as low as 0.325 eV and a room-temperature reaction rate of 3.47×106 s-1, six orders of magnitude higher than its single-ferroic derivative Cr(pyz)2. The synergistic catalysis mechanism of polarization and spin is revealed (Nat. Commun. 2025, 16, 1580). (d)Strain-engineered edge-nitrogen-doped graphene nanoribbons for 0RR: Under 13 - 14% strain, the overpotential is as low as 0.34 V, clarifying the regulation of strain on the electronic structure and adsorption behavior of active sites (Appl. Surf. Sci. 2025, 689, 162538). (e)FePc anchored on Ti02 nanotube step sites: The intrinsic magnetic field at Ti02

atomic step sites induces spin flip of Fe 3d near the Fermi level, enhancing Fe-0 covalent bonding and accelerating the ORR protonation step. This validates the spin-flip strategy for reducing the 02 activation barrier (ACS Catal. 2024, 14, 7191). (f)p-block Sb/Bi spin-polarized SACs: Defect-free Ti02 anchors Sb and Bi to form p-SACs, following Wigner spin conservation. The rate-determining step barrier for 02 activation and CO oxidation is 0.6 eV, outperforming non-magnetic, low-spin noble metal SACs (PCCP 2024, 26, 16459). (f)Dynamically confined single-atom catalysts: Single atoms are confined in porous g-C3N4/MoS2 heterojunctions, dynamically regulating charge and spin during the reaction and returning to a stable configuration post-reaction, achieving high activity (Phys. Rev. B 2023, 107, 205421).
