## 中文摘要

全球每年有50万例脊髓损伤发生，且呈逐年上升趋势。咳嗽功能受损导致呼吸系统并发症是主要死因之一。现有辅助咳嗽技术如人工吸痰、振动排痰和腹部冲击排痰等存在非生理、有创、复杂、专业性强、排痰效率低及不能提高咳嗽能力等问题。本研究团队受腹部冲击排痰技术和“铁肺负压通气”思想启发，提出了“仿自然咳嗽机制的辅助排痰软体外骨骼机器人”概念；通过建立人机耦合呼吸力学，量化机器人辅助下人体呼吸系统的动态特征，为仿生设计和智能控制提供理论基础；将人机耦合咳嗽生理机制和软体机器人技术相结合，突破基于仿呼吸肌的可变刚度材料驱动控制机理、软体材料制备工艺、人机协同控制策略等技术瓶颈，研发自然咳嗽仿生机制启发的辅助排痰软体外骨骼机器人，用于脊髓损伤等咳嗽无力患者，从而实现无创、高效、智能及连续使用的辅助咳嗽。该技术已在本团队前期研究中得到初步验证，本项目成果对降低呼吸系统并发症相关死亡率、节约医疗资源具有重要价值。

## 英文摘要

There are 500,000 cases of spinal cord injury worldwide every year, and it is increasing yearly. Respiration complications caused by impaired cough function have been considered as the primary cause of death. Existing cough assistance techniques including artificial sputum suction, vibration sputum discharge and abdominal manual thrust have a series of problems such as non-physiological, invasive, complex, highly professional, ineffective, and hard to improve cough ability. Inspired by the abdominal manual thrust technique and the “iron lung negative pressure ventilation”, we proposed the concept of “cough-inspired soft exoskeleton robot for expectoration assistance”. Based on human-robot coupling respiratory mechanics, the dynamic characteristics of the human respiratory system with robotic assistance can be quantitatively analyzed; the model also provides a theoretical basis for bionic design and intelligent control of soft exoskeleton robot. By combining of human-robot coupling cough physiological mechanism and soft robotic technology, we plan to explore the actuation principles of variable stiffness materials, structural design and material fabrication of soft materials, and human-robot coordinated control strategy. Further, we develop a cough-inspired soft exoskeleton robot for expectoration assistance for spinal cord injury patients and other patients with cough dysfunction. The proposed soft exoskeleton robot can achieve non-invasive, efficient, intelligent, and continuous use of assisted cough for patients. The feasibility of the proposed technology has been verified by our preliminary research. This technology has been initially verified in the team’s research, and the results of this project are of great value in reducing mortality related to respiratory complications and saving medical resources. And the soft exoskeleton robot is promising to reduce the mortality related to respiratory complications and save medical resources.

## 结题摘要

本项目面向呼吸辅助与康复的临床需求，整合软体机器人、膈肌超声和脑电（EEG）技术，构建“脑-机-膈”闭环呼吸辅助系统。首先，开发可穿戴软体外骨骼——吸气模块以-30 kPa真空提拉胸廓，呼气模块用400 N折纸气囊推腹升膈，同步无线IMU实时监测胸壁运动；其次，创新引入膈肌超声成像，实现机器人辅助下膈肌位移与潮气量预测误差<7%，验证健康人膈肌冲程提升1.95倍、潮气量提升2.14倍。进一步，首次提取0-2 Hz EEG低频功率、相位锁定值(PLV)和样本熵作为“自主呼吸意识”，在线识别率≥90%，使系统可按患者意图即时调整辅助水平。临床实验涵盖20名受试者（10健康+10例COPD/ALS/SCI等），结果显示患者吸气量增加276.6%，呼气量增加306.0%，PaCO₂平均下降3.1 mmHg，PaO₂上升8.7 mmHg，无不良事件。该研究实现了非侵入、 biomimetic、三重反馈的呼吸辅助新模式，为智能康复、家庭通气和未来脑控呼吸接口提供了可行范式。
