## 中文摘要

研究和开发缺氧响应的探针，利用其在肿瘤缺氧条件下的多模态成像对实现精准高效的肿瘤诊断具有重大意义。本项目拟设计并合成在肿瘤微酸环境中粒子大小可变的靶向纳米体系，用于递送PAI/MRI多模态造影剂，以达到增强其血液循环时间、靶向能力和渗透效率。该纳米体系（Au/Gd@Se@RRGD）由纳米硒包裹缺氧敏感的纳米金和手性钆配合物，表面修饰环肽cRGDy和细胞穿透肽R8作为靶向。在肿瘤微酸条件下，纳米硒水解，被包裹的Au-NO2和Gd3+配合物被释放出来并渗透进入肿瘤深层区域。而且，在缺氧环境下，Au-NO2和Gd3+配合物表面NO2基团同时被还原成为NH2，导致AuNPs聚集产生PA信号，钆配合物产生较强的MRI信号。因此，该纳米探针可用于实时监测肿瘤发展过程中缺氧的分布状况和浓度变化。此外，在缺氧条件下聚集的AuNPs和纳米硒可发挥热疗和化疗的协同抗肿瘤作用，从而实现诊疗一体化。

## 英文摘要

Hypoxia is a common and distinguished feature of various solid tumors. It has been considered as a key marker for tumor progression and requires effective methods to reliably detect and image hypoxia. It is vital that the research and development of hypoxia-responsive probes and utilize their multimodal imaging under hypoxic conditions, which could achieve accurate and efficient cancer diagnosis and treatment. In this study, we will design nanocomposites with size-switching capabilities in the acidic tumor microenvironment for surmounting multiple obstacles to deliver contrast agents for hypoxia-responsive PAI and MRI multimodal tumor hypoxia imaging, especially tackling the problem of weak tumor penetration. The nanocomposite (Au/Gd@Se@RRGD) consists of a selenium nanoparticle core (SeNPs) modified with tumor-targeting cRGDy peptide and tumor-penetrating R8 peptide on the surface. The interior consists of hypoxia-responsive gold nanoparticles (Au-NO2) and chiral gadolinium (Gd3+) complexes, which are designed to improve the sensitivity and accuracy of in vivo tumor hypoxia detection in PAI and MRI imaging respectively...The peptide-modified SeNPs is able to improve tumor penetration and actively accumulate in the tumor. Subsequently, Au-NO2 and Gd3+ complexes are released as a result of hydrolysis under the mildly acidic tumor environment and become localized in deeply-penetrated tumor regions. Under the hypoxic environment, the nitro group (NO2) on AuNPs will be reduced to amino group (NH2) by the nitroreductase (NTR) enzyme that exists widely in tumors, resulting in the aggregation of AuNPs to produce an enhanced PA signal. Until now, there are no reports on gold nanoparticle-based contrast agent for hypoxia-responsive PA imaging. At the same time, the NO2 on the Gd3+ complex is also reduced to NH2, significantly increasing its relaxation time and thus enhancing the T1 signal, leading to a higher contrast in MR imaging. The novel contrast agents can be used to investigate the distribution and changes of tumor hypoxia for in vivo PA/MR multimodal imaging. In addition, this stimulus nanocomposite exhibits near infrared plasmonic absorption features due to the aggregated AuNPs and facilitates the synergistic photothermal therapy (PTT) and chemotherapy (from SeNPs) under the guidance of PA/MR imaging, leading to integrated cancer diagnosis and treatment and improved anti-tumor efficiency. The developed hypoxia-responsive nanocomposite will pave a new way for researchers to design novel theranostic materials for precision tumor hypoxia imaging and cancer therapy.

## 结题摘要

肿瘤内部的缺氧状态与其恶性程度及抗药性密切相关。通过影像技术地能提升对缺氧程度的理解，更有效地指导临床检测和治疗，从而无创提高治疗成功率。在本研究项目中，我们成功开发了针对缺氧环境设计的金纳米粒子和手性钆配合物作为光声及核磁共振成像的诊断剂。这些材料能够有效渗透肿瘤组织，并对缺氧变化作出快速而敏感的响应。此外，我们还开发了多个多功能纳米平台，包括经过RGD多肽修饰的纳米粒子以及中空介孔有机硅结构，这些创新的设计不仅促进了药物的有效递送，并具实现有靶向性、弛豫率高、用量少和毒副作用小的光声引导光热疗法，促进诊疗一体化。些新纳米诊疗平台，为肿瘤的临床检测提供了新的视角和技术支持，提高治疗成功率，也为纳米医学领域的发展开辟了新路径。
