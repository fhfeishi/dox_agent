## 中文摘要

脑影像数据的高维性、小样本量和深度学习的黑盒特性，制约了深度学习在网络成瘾脑影像数据分析处理中的应用。本项目重点研究基于深度学习的网络成瘾脑影像数据分析方法。项目主要包括三个方面：(1)研究三维脑影像特征提取与表示算法，并设计了基于特征空间转换的深度学习分类模型，解决了三维卷积神经网络模型参数量大以及模型训练时计算能力不足的问题；(2)研究基于多任务共享特征的小样本深度学习算法，通过多任务联合训练并共享深度学习中的特征提取层，降低了单个任务对训练数据的需求；(3)研究基于深度学习的多视角共识病理特征检测算法，通过可解释性算法找出不同视角的分类依据并进行共现性分析，得到可量化评估的病理特征。本项目以期开发适用于高维性、小样本网络成瘾脑影像数据分类和病理检测算法，为网络成瘾的临床诊断和病理生理机制研究提供辅助支持。

## 英文摘要

The high dimensionality and small sample volume of brain image data, together with the black box characteristics of deep learning, greatly restrict the application of deep learning in the analysis and processing of Internet addiction. This project focuses on the analysis methods of Internet addiction based on brain image data and deep learning, which mainly includes the following three aspects: (1) to extract and represent 3D brain image features and design space-conversion-based deep learning algorithm in order to address the problems of massive parameters in the 3D convolutional neural network model and insufficiency of the computing capacity during model training process; (2) to design feature-sharing based multi-task small sample deep learning methods to reduce the training sample requirements of single task. (3) to develop multi-view consensus based Pathological feature detection algorithm to obtain the quantitatively evaluated pathological features of Internet addiction. This project aims to develop classification and pathological detection algorithms for high-dimensional, small-sample brain image data to provide auxiliary support for the clinical diagnosis and pathophysiology research of Internet addiction.
