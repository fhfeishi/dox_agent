## 中文摘要

视网膜色素变性（RP）是常见的致盲性遗传性眼病，也是影响范围最广的眼科罕见病。RP早期缺乏典型症状，病情进展隐匿且缓慢，患者不易察觉，需要视野、视网膜电图（ERG）和光学相干断层扫描（OCT）等眼科功能和影像学检查才能诊断识别。目前，这类疑难眼病的诊断治疗非常依赖于医生丰富的经验，而基层医院缺少先进设备和资深专家，导致大部分RP患者无法得到早期诊断和及时治疗，无法得到准确的遗传咨询，无法对病情的进展做长期的随访。本项目拟采用多模影像组学、深度学习等人工智能技术、以及大数据分析技术，充分融合不同模态、不同时间点、不同维度的RP临床影像、功能检查和遗传基因数据，攻关RP诊断识别、病灶定位分割、病情进展跟踪与发生发展预测等关键问题，研制开发自动化诊断与分析系统对RP进行早期预警与病情评估，并开展临床示范应用。本项目为降低诊断和随访门槛，提高RP智能化筛查和随访水平，探索新方法和新技术。

## 英文摘要

Retinitis pigmentosa (RP) is a hereditary disease that is a common cause of blindness, and is also the most influential rare ophthalmopathy. For the lack of typical symptoms of RP in early stage, the disease progresses insidiously and slowly and is difficult finding. Visual-field, electroretinogram (ERG), optical coherence tomography (OCT) and other functional and imagological examinations are necessary for the diagnosis of RP. Accurate diagnosis requires experienced ophthalmologists. However, due to the lack of advanced equipment and senior experts in primary hospitals, most RP patients cannot get early diagnosis and timely treatment, accurate genetic consultation and long-term follow-up. This research is to solve key problems such as RP diagnosis and identification, lesion location and segmentation, progress tracking and prediction. For this purpose, artificial intelligence technology including multi-modality radiomics and deep learning will be adopted to fully integrate RP clinical data of different modes, different time and different dimensions. An automatic diagnosis and analysis system is to be developed for early warning and disease assessment of RP, and clinical demonstration application will be carried out. This research is to explore new methods and technologies that can reduce barriers of diagnosis and follow-up, and improve the level of RP intelligent screening and follow-up.
