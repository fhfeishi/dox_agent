## 中文摘要

从当前全球人工智能发展情况及趋势看，可解释、可通用的下一代人工智能方法重大研究计划具有极强的创新突破性，研究内容和预期攻关目标的设计相对乐观，尽管有较好的前期科研工作基础，但从风险管理角度看，仍存在科研目标不达预期的可能性。因此，本重大研究计划立项之初便成立了指导专家组和项目管理组。为确保指导专家组能够有效指导本重大研究计划的高水平和高质量实施，指导专家组拟申请“人工智能未来发展趋势战略研究”项目支持，在本重大研究计划实施的前3年，通过专题研讨、学术交流、技术路线讨论、项目定期检查跟踪等多种方式，重点围绕本重大研究计划相关的科研领域开展战略研究，确保本重大研究计划各项子项目的基础理论、科研方向和技术路线的正确性。

## 英文摘要

Judging from the current global artificial intelligence development and trends, the major research plans for interpretable and general-purpose next-generation artificial intelligence methods have strong innovation and breakthroughs. The design of research content and expected research goals is relatively optimistic. Although there is a good foundation of preliminary scientific research work, but from the perspective of risk management, there is still the possibility that the scientific research goals will not meet expectations. Therefore, a guiding expert group and a project management group were established at the beginning of the establishment of this major research plan. In order to ensure that the guiding expert group can effectively guide the high-level and high-quality implementation of this major research plan, the guiding expert group intends to apply for the project support of "Strategic Research on the Future Development Trend of Artificial Intelligence". Various methods such as seminars, academic exchanges, technical route discussions, regular inspection and tracking of projects, etc., Focus on carrying out strategic research around the scientific research fields related to this major research plan to ensure the correctness of the basic theory, scientific research direction and technical route of each sub-project of this major research plan.
