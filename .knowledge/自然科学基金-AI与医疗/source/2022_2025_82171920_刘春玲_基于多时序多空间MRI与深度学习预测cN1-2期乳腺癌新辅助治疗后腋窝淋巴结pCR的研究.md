## 中文摘要

cN1-2期乳腺癌约占我国乳腺癌50%，其首选新辅助治疗（NAT），NAT后腋窝淋巴结（ALN）达到病理完全缓解（pCR）可免除ALN清扫；但目前无创性检查预测准确性达不到NCCN指南要求（假阴性率<10%）。申请人团队及国内外研究证实，利用深度学习可提高对乳腺癌ALN转移预测准确性，为预测cN1-2期乳腺癌NAT后ALN pCR提供新思路，但存在单次、单部位MRI特征提取信息不全面、不同维度特征融合困难等挑战导致预测准确性低。因此，本项目拟：1.基于按时序输入GRU网络的多时序原发灶及ALN MRI深度学习进行特征提取，采用Lasso回归模型进行特征筛选；2. 采用Kronecker积对MRI关键特征、临床病理分子特征进行多维特征融合、预测；3.利用内外部数据对模型进行验证，进一步提高NAT后ALN pCR预测准确性。本研究结果将为预测NAT后ALN的pCR提供精准化工具，辅助临床决策。

## 英文摘要

Patients with clinical N1-2 stage breast cancer account for about 50% of patients with local advanced breast cancer. Neoadjvant therapy (NAT) is the preferred treatment option for them. Axillary lymph node dissection (ALND) for Patients with axillary lymph node(ALN) pathological Complete Response (pCR) after NAT can be avoided. However, non-invasive methods based on clinical examination or conventional radiologic imaging cannot accurately predict ALN pCR. Our previous and other researchers' results are indicating that deep learning can extract deep imaging features from radiologic images of the breast cancers and it is feasible to predict axillary lymph node metastasis, which provides us a new idea to predict ALN pCR after NAT. However, there are still some shortcomings: 1. Most of the studies only use one primary breast cancer MRI, which can lead to extract the incomplete information; 2. MRI features and clinical, pathologic, molecular information can’t be fused accurately; Thus, the predicting accuracy is very low. Therefore, this project is going to: 1. extract multiple-time series breast primary cancers and axillary lymph nodes MRI features based on GRU net and Select key features based on Lasso regression model; 2.integrate multiple-time series, multiple spatial deep features and clinical, pathological, molecular markers to predict pCR for N1-2 stage breast cancers;3.validate the model with interior prospective and external data and increase the accuracy of the built model. The results of our project will offer a new adjunct tool for surgeons to precisely predict the pCR of N1-2 axillary lymph node after NAt and will provide individual precise treatment for patients. It will hopefully provide very valuable aid in clinical decision-making in precise medicine.

## 结题摘要

本项目针对cN1-2期乳腺癌新辅助治疗（NAT）后腋窝淋巴结（ALN）病理完全缓解（pCR）预测难、假阴性率高这一临床瓶颈问题，按计划圆满完成了研究任务。项目执行期间：1. 建立了包含NAT全周期（治疗前、早期、后期）多时点MRI影像及临床病理信息的多中心标准化数据库（N>1000）；2. 创新性地提出了基于时空交互（STI）的深度学习模型，利用Transformer和孪生网络架构，有效捕捉了肿瘤在治疗过程中的时序动态变化与空间异质性；3. 开发了基于MRI的肿瘤内异质性（ITH）量化指标及多模态融合评分系统（BCAM-pCR Score），显著提升了pCR预测的准确性（AUC提升至0.90以上）；4. 揭示了影像学特征与肿瘤免疫微环境（如TILs、CD8+ T细胞）的生物学关联。研究成果发表于 Radiology、eClinicalMedicine 等高水平期刊，为避免非必要的腋窝淋巴结清扫提供了精准、无创的辅助决策工具，具有重要的临床应用价值。
