## 中文摘要

针对传统刚性连杆康复机器人易出现“强迫性”伤害及驱动能力不足等问题，开展基于磁流变执行器的半主动柔性驱动和控制方法研究。探索磁流变执行器、电机与绳索多源混合驱动方法，并建立驱动系统的刚柔耦合动力学模型和逆动态模型。利用磁流变执行器的可控被动力矩输出特性在电机、绳索之间传递力矩，通过磁流变执行器直接控制绳索张力，能够在确保产生足够张力输出的情况下，实现高带宽力控制。由于兼具了主动执行器和被动执行器的优点，基于磁流变的半主动柔性驱动系统拥有惯性低、结构紧凑、可控性能优异和本质被动性的显著优势。在驱动系统输出稳定可靠的绳索张力基础上，针对柔性康复机器人系统存在的非线性和模型不确定性问题，提出基于径向基神经网络的不确定部分描述模型和基于患肢状态辨识的自适应控制方法，实现对不同状态患肢具有适应性的柔性康复机器人精确运动/力矢量控制，解决康复机器人缺乏适应性、控制精度不足等问题。

## 英文摘要

Aiming at the problems of "forced" injury and insufficient driving ability of traditional rehabilitation robots with rigid connecting rods, a semi-active flexible driving and control method based on magnetorheological actuator is developed. The multi-source hybrid driving method of magnetorheological actuator, motor and rope was explored, and the rigid-flexible coupling dynamic model and inverse dynamic model of the driving system were established. The controllable passive torque output characteristics of the magnetorheological actuator are used to transfer the torque between the motor and the rope, and the tension of the rope can be directly controlled by the magnetorheological actuator, which can achieve high bandwidth force control under the condition that sufficient tension output is generated. Due to the advantages of both active actuator and passive actuator, the semi-active flexible driving system based on magnetorheological rheology has the obvious advantages of low inertia, compact structure, excellent controllable performance and passive nature. Rope tension in driver output system is stable and reliable basis, based on the nonlinear flexible rehabilitation robot system and model uncertainty problems, and put forward based on RBF neural network is uncertain part description model and adaptive control method based on limb state identification and implementation of different state limb adaptable flexible rehabilitation robot movement/force vector control precision, solve the rehabilitation robots lack adaptability, such problems as insufficient control precision.
