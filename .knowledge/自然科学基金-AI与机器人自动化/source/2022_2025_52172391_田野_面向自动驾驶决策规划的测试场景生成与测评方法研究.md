## 中文摘要

决策规划是自动驾驶的大脑，决策规划在复杂交通场景中的适应性，是衡量自动驾驶智能化水平的核心指标。但由于场景覆盖度低、关键场景出现频率低、人机混驾环境下交互行为拟人度评价缺失等核心挑战，目前学界与业界尚未形成统一公开的决策规划测评平台。为实现对决策规划的可信测评，形成“场景派生-测试优化-评价体系-应用研究”的闭环框架，本课题首先依据上海自然驾驶数据，提出生成对抗网络和本体论方法，自动泛化派生新鲜场景，提升场景覆盖度；在此基础上，提出自适应搜索与重要度采样方法，突破高维参数空间关键场景搜索难题，提高关键场景出现频率，实现加速测试；提出交互行为社会性评价体系，采用社会价值取向指标可信度量决策规划的拟人化水平。总体形成面向自动驾驶决策规划的“场景可泛化、测试可加速、评价可互认”的公开虚拟测评平台，并基于同济大学驾驶模拟器和多车协同集群实验平台进行效果验证。成果为自动驾驶决策规划测评提供技术支持。

## 英文摘要

Motion planning is one of the core components of the automated vehicle. The safety test and evaluation of the motion planning component’s ability in complex traffic environments is a fundamental guarantee for the large-scale deployment of Highly Automated Vehicles (HAV). However, it lacks uniform and trustworthy methodology and platform to support expedited testing of HAV motion planning. Several challenges need to be addressed. First, testing scenarios collected by current collection approaches are of low coverage. In order to fulfill the requirement of scenario coverage, machine learning-based and domain knowledge-based methods including Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN) and Ontology are proposed. Second, in order to tackle the massive testing scenarios to find out critical and highly demanded scenarios in a shorter period of time, we establish the Adaptive Design of Experiment (ADOE) method based on Bayesian estimation to expedite the searching process. Besides, an enhanced Importance Sampling (IS) method is developed to quickly quantify the crash rate of HAV based on critical scenarios. Third, we propose the Social Value Orientation (SVO) as the interaction safety index to assess HAV’s human-like driving ability, which is a vital complement to the current functional safety and intended functional safety evaluation. The project is designed to provide a generalizable, automatic, fast, and coordinated scenario-based HAV simulation test and evaluation service. This study sheds light on the key research areas, including HAV safety diagnostic analysis technique, HAV test automation technique, and evaluation method for HAV algorithms.
