## 中文摘要

中国口腔癌的发病率居世界前列。槟榔成瘾是口腔癌变的重要阶段，因此对槟榔成瘾进行早期的预测和研判对于国民健康具有重要意义。本项目将分布式人工智能和医学脑影像相结合，以获取槟榔成瘾早期预测的脑影像特征为目的，从学科交叉、隐私保护和数据安全的角度，研究面向多任务多模态槟榔成瘾早期预测的联邦学习算法。项目的研究内容主要从三个方面展开：（1）设计面向联邦学习的数据自适应算法，提高深度学习的计算效率，解决联邦学习的计算异构问题；（2）研究面向联邦学习的领域发现与参数融合算法，消除数据异构导致的领域差异，实现联邦学习环境下的领域自适应；（3）设计面向联邦学习的多任务多模态的特征学习与知识蒸馏算法，构建多任务脑影像特征库，精准提纯槟榔成瘾早期预测的脑特征。本项目的研究成果可以为槟榔成瘾的早期预测和研判提供了新的视角和方法，具有重要的科学意义与临床价值。

## 英文摘要

The incidence of oral cancer in China ranks forefront in the world. Betel quid dependency is a crucial stage of oral cancer. Therefore, it is important to make early prediction and diagnosis of betel quid dependency for national health. To obtain brain imaging features for early prediction of betel quid dependency, this project combines distributed artificial intelligence and medical brain imaging to explore federal learning algorithm for early prediction of multi-task and multi-modal betel quid dependency for the purpose of interdisciplinary solutions, privacy protection and data security. The research content of this project mainly includes three aspects: (1) to design a data adaptive algorithm for federal learning to improve the computational efficiency and slove the computational heterogeneous issues. (2) to explore domain discovery and parameter aggregation algorithms for federal learning to eliminate domain differences caused by data heterogeneity and realize domain adaptation. (3) to design a multi-task and multi-modal feature learning and knowledge distillation algorithm for federal learning to build a multi-task brain image feature database and accurately purify brain features for early prediction of betel quid dependency. This project can provide novel perspectives and methods for early prediction and diagnosis of betel quid dependency, which has important scientific significance and clinical value.
