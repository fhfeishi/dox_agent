## 中文摘要

医疗影像数据理解是智慧医疗的关键课题。当前主流的面向图像的深度学习算法对数据有较强的依赖性，而医疗影像数据样本少、标注数据成本高等特点较一般数据更为突出，使得人工智能在医疗领域的应用仍十分有限。人工智能研究中，迁移学习是解决小数据问题的主要方法之一，利用已训练的源任务的学习经验来帮助完成小规模数据集上目标任务的学习。传统迁移学习在医疗影像数据上面临两大难点：1）不具备大规模标注数据集作为迁移的信息源；2）无法保证数据分布、任务种类不同的多种异构任务间的迁移效果。本项目针对多种医疗影像理解任务，提出一套兼具理论依据与实用性的小数据异构任务知识迁移框架。该框架借鉴人类学习新任务时高效调用过去处理多种相关问题经验的模式，通过量化任务之间的可迁移性、优化多个学习目标的层次性迁移策略以及结合领域知识的三个重要步骤，创立一种新型多任务迁移学习范式，将有望在智慧医疗领域展现出迁移学习的真正潜力。

## 英文摘要

Medical image understanding is an important area of research for intelligent medicine applications. The mainstream methods for visual recognition today are based on deep neural networks, which tend to rely heavily on having a large number of training data. Medical imaging data are often smaller in sample size and far costlier to obtain accurate annotations than natural images used in generic visual perception tasks. As a result, artificial intelligence-powered diagnosis for medical images is still in an early stage of adoption. An important paradigm for solving the small data problem is through transfer learning, which uses the experience from a known source task to help the learning of a related target task. Traditional transfer learning methods face two major challenges in medical image understanding: 1) the lack of a large-scale labeled dataset to serve as the transfer source; 2) no effective guarantee on the transfer performance between different tasks, which may differ largely in data distribution and task objectives (a.k.a. heterogenous tasks). In this project, a novel knowledge transfer framework for heterogeneous tasks with limited training data is proposed, with both theoretical support and good practical performance. This framework is inspired by how humans learn new tasks by efficiently recall knowledge from previous learning experiences on multiple related tasks.  A multi-task transfer learning paradigm will be developed through quantifying transferability between tasks, optimizing the hierarchical transfer strategy of multiple target tasks and incorporating domain knowledge, with the goal of enabling the full potential of transfer learning in intelligent medicine applications.
