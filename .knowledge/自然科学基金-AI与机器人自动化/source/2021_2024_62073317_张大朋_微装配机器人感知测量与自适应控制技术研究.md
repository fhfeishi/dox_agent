## 中文摘要

针对微零件机器人装配效率和成品率较低的问题，本项目将人工智能相关理论引入微装配机器人领域，以ICF微靶装配为应用原型，围绕机器人微装配中的感知测量和自适应控制两大科学问题，深入研究跨尺度微零件的快速识别定位与位姿测量、基于演示示教的机器人装配技能模仿学习、微装配机器人自适应控制与优化等关键技术，提出显微视觉场景下微零件的快速识别和微装配机器人示教技能的描述与重构方法、建立兼顾“在线控制”和“离线学习”的机器人装配控制模型。本项目的成功实施将有效地提升微零件的机器人装配效率和成品率，同时也为智能机器人应用提供新理论和新方法。

## 英文摘要

The micro-assembly robot exists poor quality and low efficiency, the artificial intelligence (AI) is introduced into the research, and the ICF target assembly is regarded as applicable prototype. Focusing on the two scientific issues: perception measurement and adaptive control, the research includes: 1) the rapid recognition and positioning of micro-parts and pose measurement across the depth of field; 2) the imitation study of robotic assembly technology based on the demonstration and teaching; 3) robotic self-assembly control with self-learning ability. The research proposes automatic recognition method of micro-part in the micro vision scene, the description and reconstruction method of robotic assembly skill, the robotic control model with takes both “online control” and “offline learning” into account. The successful implement of this project will effectively improve the autonomy and assembly efficiency of micro-assembly robot, also provide new theory and method for intelligent robots.
