## 中文摘要

随着多智能体规模的不断扩大以及协作任务的不断复杂，基于噪声的强化学习随机探索方式难以获得正向奖励，且探索过程未考虑智能体间的交互信息，这也就使得多智能体系统难以实现协同探索，进而导致了策略学习缓慢、训练难收敛等问题，因此研究多智能体的协同探索机制是具有实际意义的。本项目面向多智能体协作任务，设计基于知识引导的多智能体协同探索机制，利用和改进多智能体强化学习算法获取协作策略。项目首先研究基于专家示教数据和协同逻辑规则的探索范围构建方法，实现协同探索范围的动态调节。为了有效评估多智能体系统的探索效率，研究基于反事实信息的协同探索奖励设计方法以及探索策略更新算法。在上述探索策略的基础上，进一步考虑复杂任务场景下的多智能体协作问题，研究基于分层结构和值分解网络的多智能体强化学习框架。本项目的开展有益于推动多智能体强化学习理论的发展，同时也可为强化学习算法在实际场景中的应用提供技术支持。

## 英文摘要

With the rapid growing of the scale of multi-agent systems and the increasing complexity of cooperative tasks, it is hard for noise-based exploration methods to obtain positive rewards, and the exploration process does not consider the interaction information between agents, which makes it difficult for multi-agent systems to achieve cooperative exploration, and then leads to poor learning performance or divergence of the training process. Hence, the exploration mechanism is of great importance. This project focuses on the multi-agent cooperative tasks, and designs a knowledge-guided cooperative exploration mechanism, and then develops a multi-agent reinforcement learning algorithm to obtain cooperative strategies. The project first studies the method of using expert demonstration to build the exploration range, and realizes the adaptive mechanism for the cooperative exploration range. In order to effectively evaluate the exploration efficiency of multi-agent systems, a reward shaping method based on counterfactual information is studied, and a learning-based architecture is developed for obtaining the cooperative exploration strategy. Based on the above exploration strategy, the multi-agent cooperative task in the complex environment is further considered, and a multi-agent reinforcement learning framework based on the hierarchical structure and the value decomposition network is studied. The research of this project has scientific significance for the development of multi-agent reinforcement learning theory, and also provides technical support for the application of reinforcement learning algorithms in practical scenarios.
