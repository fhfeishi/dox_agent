## 中文摘要

EGFR突变NSCLC脑转移发生率高，对TKI治疗反应的异质性大，如何实现精准诊治是亟待解决的临床难题。我们前期利用NGS检测及影像组学等多维信息初步构建了脑转移演进关键节点（脑转移发生、TKIs脑转移耐药和/或全身耐药）的预测模型，提示EGFR突变NSCLC脑转移的发生发展有独特的演进规律。充分利用多维异构信息，精准预测疾病转归，在关键节点发生之前给予特异性精准治疗，是解决脑转移疗效差的必由之路。本项目拟利用已建立的EGFR突变NSCLC脑转移及靶向治疗临床数据库，应用神经网络、机器学习等智能组学技术深度融合NGS检测和影像组学等多维纵向异构信息，构建脑转移发生风险、局部和/或全身耐药的动态预测模型，并据此筛选目标人群采集肿瘤组织建立PDX动物模型，验证上述模型的可行性和准确性。本项目有望实现脑转移演进规律的动态精准分析，为实现EGFR突变NSCLC脑转移的个体化治疗打下理论和实践基础。

## 英文摘要

The incidence of brain metastasis is high in epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) mutation non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) patients, and the response to tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs) treatment is heterogeneous. How to administrate precision treatment is an urgent challenge. Previously, we constructed several models by next-generation sequencing (NGS) and CT radiomics to predict the key steps in the evolution progress of brain metastasis, i.e. the presence of brain metastases, TKIs resistance of brain metastases, and/or systemic TKIs resistance. We found that the occurrence and development of EGFR mutation NSCLC brain metastasis has its own unique evolution rules. Making full use of the multi-source isomerous data, accurately predicting the outcome, and implementing precision treatment before progressing to the key points of evolution process is the only way to resolve the poor prognosis of brain metastases. The purpose of this project is to use our previously clinical database of EGFR mutation NSCLC patients with brain metastasis and targeted therapy, and apply artificial intelligent technology to deeply integrate multi-source longitudinal isomerous information such as NGS gene profile and CT & MR radiomics, to construct dynamic prediction models of brain metastasis risk and local and/or systemic drug resistance by neural network and machine learning, and to verify these models by patient-derived xenograft mice models from selected patients. This project is expected to achieve dynamic and accurate analysis of the evolution rule of brain metastasis, and provide a theoretical and practical foundation for the individual treatment of EGFR mutation NSCLC brain metastasis.

## 结题摘要

EGFR突变NSCLC脑转移发生率高，疗效异质性大。本项目旨在构建其动态演进的智能预测模型，以指导精准干预。研究围绕脑转移发生、靶向治疗反应及耐药等关键节点，系统开展了多模态影像组学与临床-基因组信息的融合分析。通过整合CT、PET/CT及MRI影像组学特征，并结合临床与基因数据，成功构建了一系列脑转移风险、EGFR-TKI疗效及颅内无进展生存期的预测模型。关键结果显示，融合模型预测性能优异，MRI影像组学可有效预测EGFR-TKI疗效，PET/CT肿瘤播散特征是晚期NSCLC生存的独立预后因素。治疗分析表明，三代EGFR-TKI一线治疗优于序贯使用，耐药后免疫联合方案可改善生存。本项目通过多中心大样本研究，构建了具有临床潜力的智能预测工具，初步阐明了EGFR突变肺癌脑转移的演进规律，为个体化治疗提供了新依据，推动了影像组学与人工智能在肺癌精准医疗中的转化应用。
