## 中文摘要

肝移植是肝细胞癌重要的治疗手段，我院研究结果显示肝移植患者中多结节型肝癌患者占到48%。多结节型肝癌可分为多中心起源（MO）及肝内转移（IM）两种形式。IM相对于MO具有明显的早期复发特点，如能在术前即对其进行精准评价，则对于肝移植适应证的选择将有着重大的意义。基于我们团队提出的“相似度”这一理论及影像组学在区分HCC细微特征中的独到应用价值，本研究将以术后的病理及基因测序技术为金标准，对多结节型肝癌患者肝移植术前行多模态MR扫描，利用“相似度”树状图和影像组学判定多个结节的相似度，建立术前诊断多结节型肝癌起源方式的临床影像组学模型。此外，对我院提出的肝移植“上海复旦标准”进行优化，以期在肝移植前即判定多结节型肝癌的起源方式，从而将IM排除在肝移植适应证外，同时扩大MO的适用范围，并进一步探讨国内众多肝移植标准扩大其适用范围的初步机制。

## 英文摘要

Liver transplantation is an important treatment for hepatocellular carcinoma, and the findings show that 48% of patients with liver transplantation have multifocal hepatocellular carcinoma in our hospital. Multifocal hepatocellular carcinoma can be classified into two forms: multi-centered origin (MO) and intrahepatic metastasis (IM). Compared with MO, IM has significant characteristics of early recurrence, and if it can be evaluated accurately before surgery, it will be of great significance to the choice of liver transplantation indication. Based on the theory of "similarity" proposed by our team and the unique application value of radiomics in distinguishing the subtle features of HCC, this study will use postoperative pathology and gene sequencing technology as the gold standard to establish a clinical radiomics model for preoperative diagnosis of the origin of multifocal hepatocellular carcinoma, performing multi-modal MR scans for the patients with multifocal hepatocellular carcinoma before liver transplantation, and determining the similarity of multiple nodules by "similarity" dendrograms and radiomics. In addition, we also optimize the "Shanghai Fudan Standards" for liver transplantation proposed by our hospital, in order to determine the origin of multifocal hepatocellular carcinoma before liver transplantation, so as to exclude the IM patients from the indications for liver transplantation and expand the scope of application for the MO patients. The preliminary mechanism of many domestic liver transplantation standards to expand their scope of application is furtherly investigated.

## 结题摘要

多结节型肝癌可分为多中心起源（MO）及肝内转移（IM）两种形式。IM相对于MO具有明显早期复发的特点，如能在术前对其进行精准评价，则对于肝移植适应症的选择将有着重大的意义。本课题主要围绕着多结节型肝癌展开研究，利用传统影像学征象分析及生境分析等手段探讨多结节型肝癌的预后因素。研究内容与成果主要包括以下四个方面：1)l利用术前临床影像学变量在多结节型HCC患者中构建并验证了MVI预测模型。2）利用术前临床影像学变量，在超出米兰标准的多结节型肝细胞癌患者中构建术后RFS和OS的预测模型。3）利生境分析技术，在双结节型HCC患者中构建MVI预测模型。本研究结果对于多结节型HCC患者重要的生物标志物（MVI）预测及预后预测具有重要价值。
