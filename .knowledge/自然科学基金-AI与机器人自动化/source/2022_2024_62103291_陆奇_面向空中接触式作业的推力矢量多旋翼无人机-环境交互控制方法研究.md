## 中文摘要

工业无人机系统的接触式作业控制技术已成为近年来国际学术研究的热点前沿。新型推力矢量多旋翼无人机可六自由度独立控制的特点解决了常规推力共线多旋翼无人机的欠驱动问题，且可与单少自由度作业工具构成结构简单、控制稳定、互动灵巧的工业无人机系统；然而，其作业过程中存在的平转动耦合效应、科氏力不确定性、执行机构动态极限及多源外界扰动影响了系统动态响应性能和主动交互品质。本项目基于对偶四元数方法一体化建模无人机位姿运动，探索其六自由度跟踪控制机理，建立全局无奇异且唯一的位姿耦合控制算法；进而利用动态寻优方法探究闭环控制分配方案，实现简洁计算、实时反馈、动态分配的求解机制；同时采用不确定扰动估计器理论观测阵风、模型不确定性等多因素扰动，用于确保无人机-环境交互控制的接触力精确性，并结合异源扰动场景飞行实验进行关键技术验证。项目成果可为推力矢量多旋翼无人机系统提供空中接触式作业控制理论和方法支撑。

## 英文摘要

The contact-based operation control technology of the industrial UAV system has become a popular area and frontier for academic research in recent years. The novel thrust vectoring multi-rotor UAV which can independently control six degrees of freedom solves the under-actuation problem of conventional collinear-thrust multi-rotor UAVs. It can be combined with single-degree-of-freedom operation tools to construct the industrial UAV system with the benefits of simple structure, stable control, and smart interaction. However, the translational-rotational coupling effect, Coriolis force uncertainty, actuators’ dynamics limit and multi-source external disturbances in its operation process deteriorate the system’s dynamic response performance and active interaction quality. This research proposal advocates the dual quaternion method to model the UAV's pose integrally with one variable while exploring the six-degree-of-freedom tracking control mechanism with the establishment of a globally non-singular and unique position-orientation-coupling control algorithm. Then the dynamic optimum seeking method is employed to investigate the closed-loop control allocation scheme, while realizing the solution mechanism with efficient computation, real-time feedback, and dynamic allocation properties. Meanwhile, the uncertainty and disturbance estimator theory is utilized to observe the multi-factor disturbances, such as wind gusts and model uncertainties and to ensure the accurate contact force control for the UAV-environment interaction. The developed key technologies will be validated with the flight experiments of heterogeneous disturbance scenarios. The research results can provide theoretical foundation and technical support for the aerial contact-based operation control of the thrust vectoring multi-rotor UAV system.

## 结题摘要

该项目围绕推力矢量多旋翼无人机的运动控制、控制分配和接触力控制方法展开研究，旨在推动全向运动推力矢量多旋翼无人机在接触式作业中的应用。项目按计划完成了三个年度的研究任务，主要包括基于对偶四元数的六自由度一体化跟踪控制机理研究、闭环动态控制分配方法研究以及多因素扰动下的接触力精准控制方法研究。项目成功研制了全向运动推力矢量多旋翼无人机样机，并通过实验验证了所提出控制方法的有效性和优越性。项目成果包括发表SCI论文5篇、EI论文4篇，授权国家发明/实用新型专利4项，并获得IEEE International Conference on Unmanned Systems最佳论文奖。项目负责人获批天府峨眉计划青年人才，并培养了多名研究生和本科生。后续将结合项目研究内容开展无人机高空接触式作业和接触式巡检等方面的技术转化和产业应用。
