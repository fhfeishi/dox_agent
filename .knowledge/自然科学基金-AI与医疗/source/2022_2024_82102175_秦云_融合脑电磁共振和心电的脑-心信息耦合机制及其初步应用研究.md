## 中文摘要

大脑与心脏之间存在着复杂的相互作用，两者之间的交互已经引起了神经科学和临床领域的广泛关注。大脑内的中央自主网络在调节心脏自主活动中扮演了重要角色，该网络的异常调节对颞叶癫痫等神经系统疾病的心脏自主活动障碍有重要的影响。然而，由于对脑-心之间的信息耦合缺乏系统的研究，大脑对心脏是如何实现调节的仍然是一个开放未解决的问题。因此，本项目发展基于脑电、磁共振和心电的多模态信息融合方法，克服传统研究中脑功能与心脏功能独立研究的不足，重点考察心率变异性相关的中央自主网络关键脑区和关键环路，建立个体化脑电和心电的映射关系，努力阐明脑-心信息耦合机制；并在此基础上以颞叶癫痫患者为研究对象，探究颞叶癫痫大脑对心率变异性的异常调节，实现基于脑-心信息耦合的差异化人群识别，对于发展基于脑-心交互的癫痫诊断和干预调控技术具有理论、方法方面的意义和潜在的临床价值。

## 英文摘要

There are complex interactions between the brain and the heart, and the study of brain-heart interaction has attracted widespread attention in neuroscience and clinical fields. The central autonomic network（CAN）in the brain plays an important role in regulating the autonomic activity of the heart. The abnormal regulation of this network has an important influence on the cardiac autonomic dysfunction in neurological diseases such as temporal lobe epilepsy. However, due to the lack of systematic research on the information coupling between the brain and the heart, how the brain regulates the heart is still an open and unresolved problem. Therefore, this project develops the multi-modal fusion methods of EEG-fMRI and ECG, overcomes the shortcomings of independent research on brain function and heart function in traditional research, emphasizes the key brain areas and connections related to heart rate variability in CAN, explores the individual projection between EEG and ECG，to clarify the brain-heart information coupling mechanism. Then, on this basis, taking patients with temporal lobe epilepsy as the research object, we explore the abnormal regulation of CAN on heart rate variability, and realize the recognition of differentiated groups based on the brain-heart information coupling, thus assisting in the diagnosis and development of brain-heart interaction-based non-invasive modulation and intervention technology for temporal lobe epilepsy, which has important theoretical and methodological significance and potential clinical application values.
