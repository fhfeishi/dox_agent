## 中文摘要

对进展期胃癌是否应在手术前行新辅助化疗，目前仍有争议。研究表明，获益者仅限于对化疗敏感的患者。然而目前缺乏在治疗前预测化疗敏感性的工具，为解决此科学问题，本课题组在前期实验中，通过影像组学大数据分析，建立了胃癌新辅助化疗后病理缓解等级的预测模型，准确率达76%。现拟将研究深入推进，以曾接受新辅助化疗+手术综合治疗的进展期胃癌患者为研究对象，应用卷积神经网络模型，对干预前胃镜活检组织的全切片数字病理图像进行深度学习，从海量的数字病理图像中挖掘与化疗敏感性，治疗结局相关的信息，以化疗后影像学、病理学肿瘤退缩等级、无疾病生存期、总生存期为结局变量，分别建立疗效、生存预测模型，并引入外部数据进行模型验证，旨在为临床提供一个实用、准确的进展期胃癌围手术期精准决策辅助工具。

## 英文摘要

Gastric cancer is the fifth most common malignancy in the world and the third leading cause of cancer-related death. The majority of patients are diagnosed at an advanced stage with poor prognosis. In recent years, neoadjuvant chemotherapy (NAC) plus subsequent radical gastrectomy has become a popular treatment modality for advanced gastric cancer (AGC). Some scholars stated that NAC could result in tumor downstaging and a higher curative resection rate and may eventually prolong survival for AGC patients. Some other trials stated that NAC failed to offer any survival benefit. Moreover, well-designed prospective RCTs are still lacking. Thus, the benefit and necessity of NAC remain controversial. Previous studies have found that the survival benefit of NAC vastly depends on the pathological response of the tumor. Those with major pathological responses gained more survival benefit than others. However, for those with a minor response, NAC offers no survival benefit but only toxicity and the risk of tumor progression during chemotherapy that may hinder surgical resection. Thus, to achieve personalized precision medicine, a pre-intervention prediction model to identify major responders and minor responders is needed..To solve this problem, in the previous work, we have established a machine learning model based on radiomic features to predict responses to NAC, harvesting an accuracy of 76%. In this study, we aim to establish a digital pathological slides based deep learning model to predict responses to NAC. We plan to collect the pre-intervention biopsy pathological slides of the primary tumor lesions, identify the microscopic visual features that are related to the final pathological/radiological response to NAC and prognosis, and eventually develop a deep learning model that can predict sensitivity to NAC of the tumor before any intervention set in. This tool will serve as a practical too for clinical decision making and help realize precision medicine for AGC patients.

## 结题摘要

针对进展期胃癌新辅助化疗（NAC）疗效个体差异大、缺乏精准预测工具的临床难题，本项目开展了基于人工智能的系统性研究。首先，通过匹配队列生存分析证实了病理完全缓解（pCR）是NAC患者获得长期生存获益的决定性因素，确立了疗效预测的临床价值。核心工作中，课题组构建了包含静脉期CT影像与胃镜活检全切片数字病理图像的多模态临床数据库，利用特征工程提取了高通量影像组学与病理组学特征，结合LASSO回归与LightGBM算法，建立了Radio-Pathomic多模态融合预测模型。该模型在验证集中预测pCR的AUC达0.814，性能较佳。在此基础上，项目进一步拓展了治疗优化与术后监测研究：证实了联合免疫检查点抑制剂可将NAC的pCR率从13.7%显著提升至29.6%；基于淋巴结转移规律论证了胃上部癌新辅助治疗后全胃切除的必要性；创新性引入K-means无监督机器学习算法对肿瘤标志物（CEA、CA125）进行动态轨迹聚类分析，发现肿瘤标志物“高轨迹组”的复发风险较高，且预测效能高于传统的静态阈值，建立了新的术后复发监测预警工具。本项目共发表SCI论文7篇，为进展期胃癌从疗效预测、策略优化到预后动态监测的精准诊疗提供了理论依据和数据支撑。
