## 中文摘要

肝癌是我国常见的恶性肿瘤之一，死亡率高。近年来，免疫治疗快速发展，显著改善了中晚期肝癌的预后。但是，PD-1抗体治疗肝癌的有效率仍较低，肝癌对免疫治疗具有一定的耐药率。目前，仍缺乏有效的PD-1抗体疗效预测模型。在前期研究中，我们成功建立人类肝癌细胞系文库、肝癌类器官体模型。类器官体模型保持了原有组织的细胞成分和空间构象，因此对肿瘤细胞-免疫细胞相互作用的分析具有独特的优势，由此推测它可能用于PD-1抗体免疫治疗疗效预测。本课题拟在建立类器官体模型的基础上，验证该模型与原发肿瘤基因表型和免疫表型的一致性，评价PD-1抗体体外应答与临床应答的一致性，探索该模型用于PD-1抗体疗效预测的价值。本课题有助于揭示肝癌免疫治疗耐药的分子机制，寻找提示免疫治疗响应的敏感性生物学标志物，寻找增强免疫治疗疗效的靶点，为中晚期肝癌的个体化治疗提供指导。

## 英文摘要

Targeting therapy and immunotherapy has improved the prognosis and become the cornerstones for the treatment of advanced stage hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). We have observed remarkable successes with complete remission by PD-1 antibody treatment clinically, but the objective response rate of immunotherapy by PD-1 blockade was relatively low. Previous studies have showed the relevance of the clinical response of immunotherapy with genotype (mismatch repair machinery, microsatellite Instability, and tumor mutation burden) and immunophenotype (expression of PD-1/PDL-1 based on immunohistochemistry, expression of neoantigen and antigen processing molecules, and infiltration of immune cells). None of them has been clinically validated. Therefore, the lack of an efficient prediction model of PD-1 blockade therapy has limited its clinical application. Recently, organoid based co-culture models, which could retain the complexity of tumor microenvironment, have showed superiority than conventional ex vivo chemosensitivity assays without reconstitution of tumor infiltrating immune cells. Our previous works have focused on the pharmacogenomic study of HCC cell lines. Meanwhile, 3D organoid models have also been established by our team. This project is planned to generate an organoid based co-culture model of tumor infiltrating immune cells and tumor cells for predicting clinical responses of PD-1 immunotherapy. The organoids are prepared from patients with advanced stage HCC before starting PD-1 antibody treatment. The characteristics of immune microenvironment of hepatocellular carcinoma from both genotypical and immunophenotypical dimensions are evaluated initially. The prediction validity of the organoid model is compared with the conventional 2D co-culture model. The interaction mechanism of drug, genotype and immunophenotype is then interrogated to find out potential targets for combination therapies. The organoid based co-culture model may provide a useful platform for predicting the response of PD-1 immunotherapy, unraveling novel therapies to overcome resistance, and ultimately guiding precision medication for HCC patients.

## 结题摘要

本课题采用回顾性研究方法对肝癌免疫治疗耐药或敏感病例分组进行驱动基因差异化分析，发现11q13共扩增患者是肝癌免疫耐药或超进展的高危人群，Foxp3+Treg细胞密度增加可能是潜在机制之一，5个显著差异表达的mRNA（REG3A、SLC2A5、ITGA6、FGF1和CRYBG2）与PD1敏感性密切相关；GO和KEGG富集分析，发现其在MAPK均有较强富集；通过开展前瞻性肝癌新辅助免疫治疗研究，中期结果显示可以显著提高肝癌合并门脉癌栓患者RFS和OS，敏感组的免疫微环境呈现TMEII和III型特征，且Treg和NK细胞比例低，TGFβR1表达下降；通过构建肝癌类器官体模型制备优化体系与验证，对典型临床病例同步开展的类器官模型药物反应筛查结果与临床一致。研究结果为肝癌免疫治疗高危或敏感人群的识别提供了新的生物学标记，为肝癌精准免疫治疗提供了新的手段。
