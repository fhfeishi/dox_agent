## 中文摘要

胶质瘤是严重危害人类健康的重大脑疾病，部分分子标记物是胶质瘤诊断及预后分析的重要因素，我们前期研究表明磁共振影像组学特征能够反映脑胶质瘤的关键分子生物学特征，但准确率仍需提升。影像学和组织病理学从不同角度反映脑胶质瘤的特性，融合二者有可能提高分子标记物预测准确性。我们推测影像组学结合组织病理学能够最大限度反映脑胶质瘤关键分子标志物特征，并且通过挖掘二者之间的关联，可实现融合组织病理学先验的影像组学无创脑胶质瘤诊断、治疗及预后评估功能。本研究拟收集200例脑胶质瘤患者磁共振影像及病理信息，利用深度学习方法，包括改进的卷积神经网络、多模融合分类器等，构建高效、客观的磁共振影像学融合组织病理学对脑胶质瘤关键分子标志物信息的预测模型，并进一步运用生成对抗网络，建立融合组织病理先验的影像独立预测脑胶质瘤分子标志物信息模型，最终实现对脑胶质瘤定量诊断及疗效评估。

## 英文摘要

Glioma is a brain disease that endangers people's health. Some molecular biomarkers are important factors for evaluating the diagnosis and prognosis of glioma. Our previous studies have shown that the radiomic characteristics of magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) could reflect the key molecular biomarkers of glioma, but the accuracy is still not satisfactory. MRI and histopathological examination reflect the characteristics of glioma from different angles. Combination of the two methods for improving the prediction accuracy of molecular biomarkers could be an important direction for the scientific research and clinical work of glioma. We speculate that MRI radiomics combined with histopathology could best reflect the characteristics of key molecular biomarkers of glioma. And by investing the relationship between those two methods, with a priori knowledge of histopathology, MRI radiomics could realize the non-invasive evaluation of glioma diagnosis and prognosis. This study intends to collect MRI and histopathological information of 200 glioma patients, and uses the deep learning method, including improved Convolution Neural Network and Multimodal Fusion Classifier, to construct a high-precision and objective MRI radiomics combined histopathology model for the key molecular biomarkers prediction. Generative Adversarial Net model is further used to produce an independent MRI radiomics prediction model for the glioma molecular biomarker with a priori knowledge of histopathology. Our study aims in achieving accurate quantitative diagnosis and evaluation of glioma, in hope of providing a basis for making clinical decisions and improving patient outcomes.
