## 中文摘要

无线光通信是实现水下高速数据传输的核心技术。能否在水下不确定性环境中动态维护网络拓扑，实时协调有限资源，搭建具有自组织、自愈能力的水下无线光通信网络，是支撑海洋信息探测、采集与传输的关键技术。由于光的定向传输特点，加之受到水下不确定性环境影响，无线光通信网络节点拓扑动态性强，分布式环境下节点之间协同行为复杂，使得现有技术无法直接应用。本项目旨在利用多智能体强化学习的技术特点，突破不确定性环境下分布式网络拓扑生成、动态资源调度等关键技术瓶颈，促进水下无线光通信技术的基础研究成果走向应用。本项目首先设计基于柔性测度的网络结构评价模型，研究在不确定性环境下稳定网络拓扑构建方法；然后，设计基于多智能体强化学习的主动式光通信智能体，研发分布式环境下网络部署与动态节点接入方法，以及不确定性环境下资源调度策略；最后，融合研究成果开发水下无线光通信自组网原型，在实际浅海环境进行有效性验证。

## 英文摘要

Optical wireless communication is the core technology for underwater high-speed data transmission. In an uncertain underwater environment, how to reconstruct the network topology  and resources rescheduling dynamically is very important. These key technologies can help us build an underwater optical wireless ad-hoc network with self-organization and self-healing capabilities. They will promote the development of marine information detection, collection and transmission. Because of the directional transmission characteristic of optical wireless communication, and the effects of the uncertain underwater environment, the links between optical access points are constantly changing. Furthermore, the cooperative behavior between ad-hoc nodes becomes very complicated. The above reasons make the existing wireless network communication technologies not directly applicable. In this project, we focus on the researches of multi-agent reinforcement learning, break through some key technical bottlenecks, such as distributed network topology optimization, dynamic resources rescheduling ect., build an intelligent ad-hoc network of underwater optical wireless communication. Promote the development of underwater optical wireless communication in practical problems. First, we design a mathematical model to evaluate the structural flexibility of a network, develop an algorithm to optimize the stability of the network topology under uncertainty. Then, we design an optical wireless communication agent based on multi-agent reinforcement learning, develop a distributed network topology construction algorithm, an online reconstruction algorithm, and a resource scheduling strategy in the uncertain environment. Finally, based on the above research results,  we develop a prototype of an underwater optical wireless ad-hoc network, to verify the effectiveness in the practical shallow sea environment.
