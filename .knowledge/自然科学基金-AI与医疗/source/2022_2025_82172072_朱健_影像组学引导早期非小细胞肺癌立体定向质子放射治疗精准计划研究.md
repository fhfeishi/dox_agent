## 中文摘要

立体定向放射治疗（SBRT）对治愈早期恶性肿瘤疗效显著。相比光子（X线），质子放疗在提升杀灭肿瘤生物效应的同时显著减少并发症。然而，长期沿用的“质子生物效应剂量（BED）≈光子BED×1.1”估算方式受到越来越多质疑，且更高的生物效应使得肿瘤内部生物异质性产生更复杂变化。前期研究发现，从人体解剖影像提取的组学特征与肿瘤内部及周围器官生物异质性显著相关，若结合多模态微观影像，有望及时捕捉肿瘤内部生物功能亚区空间结构动态变化。就此提出：针对非小细胞肺癌，首先在细胞水平建立质子SBRT离体生物效应剂量换算模型；然后以荷瘤鼠照射后肿瘤影像组学特征修正，得到质子SBRT在体生物效应剂量换算模型；进而以生物等效方案实施照射，基于影像组学特征和有监督的机器学习，建立质子SBRT疗程中肿瘤生物功能亚区域变化预测模型，有针对性提升照射剂量，引导制定“量体裁衣”的质子SBRT放疗计划，提升治疗获益。

## 英文摘要

Stereotactic body radiotherapy (SBRT) is effective to cure early malignant tumors. Compared with photon (X-ray), proton radiotherapy can enhance the biological effect of killing tumor and significantly reduce complications. The long-term estimation method of "proton biological effect dose (BED) ≈ photon BED × 1.1" has been criticized more and more, and the higher biological effect makes the biological heterogeneity within the tumor more complex, which is an problem to be solved urgently in the development of proton radiotherapy. It has been found, in the previous studies, that the radiomics features extracted from human anatomical images are significantly related to the biological heterogeneity of the tumor and its surrounding organs. It is expected to capture the dynamic changes of the spatial location of the biological functional subregions within the tumor in time, if the microscopic multi-modality images are involved. Therefore, we put forward ‘the precise treatment planning of proton SBRT based on radiomics’: aiming at the non-small-cell lung cancer, the in-vitro BED conversion model for proton SBRT is firstly established at the cellular level, and then revision is performed based on the radiomics features exacted from the irradiated tumor-bearing mice to obtain the in-vivo BED conversion model for proton SBRT. Based on the radiomics features and supervised machine learning, the prediction model of tumor biological functional subregion changes during the treatment course of proton SBRT is established to guide the individual treatment plan for proton SBRT, so as to enhance the therapeutic benefits.
