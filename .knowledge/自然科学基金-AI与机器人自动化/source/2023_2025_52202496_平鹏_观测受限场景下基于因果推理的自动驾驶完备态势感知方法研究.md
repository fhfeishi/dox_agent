## 中文摘要

对道路环境的充分态势感知是自动驾驶车辆感知潜在道路风险并形成安全行为决策的关键。传统道路环境感知方法缺乏挖掘和利用观测目标状态演变因果常识的能力，难以对观测受限目标进行持续感知，使得观测受限场景下态势感知存在盲区，对行车安全构成了极大威胁。为此，本项目拟开展基于因果推理的态势感知方法研究，首先建立目标行为-可观测性演变因果知识模型，获取观测受限场景演变特征和规律；然后在因果知识驱动下，对非线性因果推理机制进行探索，建立动态化因果与或图模型，实现观测受限目标状态连续跟踪；最后通过多目标因果推理差异性融合，揭示观测受限对行车安全的影响机理，建立道路风险预测方法。本项目旨在赋予自动驾驶系统“执因索果”的道路环境感知能力，从而突破现有态势感知方法需建立在场景完全观测上的限制，为牵引自动驾驶由相关性态势感知向推理性态势感知的发展提供理论支撑，对自动驾驶感知系统的创新设计与优化验证具有重要意义。

## 英文摘要

Reliable situational awareness of the road environment is crucial for autonomous vehicles to perceive potential road risks and make safe behavior strategy. Traditional road environment perception methods lack the ability to mine and utilize the causal knowledge of the state evolution of observation targets, so it is difficult to perceive occluded targets continuously. This makes situation awareness have blind spots under restricted observation which constitutes a great threat to driving safety. To this end, this project intends to carry out research on situational awareness methods based on causal reasoning. First, a target behavior-observability evolution causal knowledge model would be established to obtain the evolution feature and pattern of the observation restricted road scene. Then, driven by the causal knowledge, the nonlinear causal reasoning mechanism is explored. A dynamic causal and-or graph model is constructed to track the state of restricted observation targets. Finally, by fusing multi-objective causal reasoning models heterogeneously, the mechanism of the influence of restricted observation on driving safety is revealed, and a driving risk prediction method is established. This project aims to give the autonomous driving system the ability to “obey the cause and find the effect”, thereby breaking the limitation of the existing situational awareness that needs to be obtained on the complete observation of the scene. What’s more, the research could further promote the development of autonomous driving from relevant situational awareness to reasonable situational awareness. Relevant research results are of great significance to promote the innovative design and optimization verification of autonomous driving perception systems.
