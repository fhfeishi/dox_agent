## 中文摘要

心血管疾病是全球的头号死因，心脏疾病作为典型的心血管疾病严重地威胁着人类健康。心音听诊作为一种非侵入、方便且廉价的手段，可有效地用于心脏疾病的辅助诊断。针对我国医疗资源分布不均的现状和心音听诊准确度亟待提高的问题，本项目拟挖掘心脏疾病与心音信号之间的本征联系，构建一套精确的计算机辅助心脏疾病诊断的理论与方法，拟开展的主要研究内容包括：1）大规模成人-婴幼儿心音数据库以及心音-心电-超声影像联合的多模态数据库构建；2）基于回环残差U型CNN和隐半马尔可夫模型的心音分割；3）基于CNN与RNN串并联、因果卷积神经网络和残差注意力机制的心音大数据深度学习与决策模型及其可解释性机理；4）基于多模态数据特征集成学习、融合学习的协同决策方法。通过本项目的研究有望建立完善公开的心音数据库，构建基于心音大数据深度学习的心脏疾病辅助诊断系统，为我国心脏疾病的预测预防提供理论与技术支撑。

## 英文摘要

Cardiovascular disease is the major cause of mortality worldwide. Heart disease as a typical cardiovascular disease, seriously threatens physical health of human. As a noninvasive, convenient and inexpensive approach, heart sound auscultation can be utilized efficiently for heart disease diagnosis. Based on current Chinese situation of uneven distribution of medical resources, and in order to improve the accuracy of heart sound auscultation, this project aims to propose a series of theories and methods for accurate computer-aided heart disease diagnosis via investigating the intrinsic relationship between heart diseases and heart sounds. The main research contents of this project are as follows: 1) Construction of the large-scale adult-pediatrics heart sound database and the PCG-ECG-UCG joint multi-modal database; 2) Heart sound segmentation based on the ringed residual U-Net and Hidden semi-Markov Model; 3) Deep learning and decision models of heart sound big data, as well as the interpretability of these models based on CNN-RNN ensemble models, causal CNN and residual attention mechanism; 4) Synergistic learning and decision models based on multi-modal features, ensemble learning and integrated learning. Through this project, a comprehensive large-scale publicly available heart sound database is expected to be established. Additionally, a computer-aided heart disease diagnosis system based on the heart sound big data and deep leaning techniques are also expected to be constructed, which provides both theoretical and technical supports for the predication and detection of heart diseases in China.
