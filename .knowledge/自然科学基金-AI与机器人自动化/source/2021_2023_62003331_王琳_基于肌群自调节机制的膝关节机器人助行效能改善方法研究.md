## 中文摘要

能为行走障碍者提供高效助力的助行机器人是国际学术前沿。目前，助行机器人膝关节设计与人体关节相比，结构功能差异大、力学驱动不等效，导致穿戴者代谢能耗高、助力效率低。本项目拟通过探索膝关节肌群自调节机制，揭示自调节对自然步态的影响机理，优化机器人关节驱动设计，寻求改善助行机器人助力效能的方法。本项目利用医学影像设备与体表电生理采集系统，获取高精度人体膝关节实时骨骼运动、肌群形态、神经肌肉电生理等信息；开展关节结构三维建模与运动学仿真，预测关节内部力学传递方式，识别对行走步态起关键作用的肌群及组件，量化靶向肌群间的协同作用；优化机器人关节驱动单元的力-位设计，使助行机器人关节重现人体膝关节的生物力学运动特性，并在助力机器人上开展个性化实验验证。本项目的顺利实施有望为基于人体关节肌群自调节机制的助行机器人关节设计提供理论依据，实现个性化自调节功能的仿生代偿，对助行机器人的效能提升有指导意义。

## 英文摘要

A walking robot that can provide effective assistance for people with walking disabilities is an international academic frontier. At present, the design of the knee joint exoskeleton is not matched the self-tunning function as same as the human joints. There are large differences in functions and structures between the human joints and machine joints, also the actuation between them can not be converted equivalently. This costs wearer more metabolic, and leads low working efficiency. This project is to study the health human knee joints by using medical imaging equipment and electrophysiological acquisition system to obtain high-precision real-time dynamic bone kinematics information, musculoskeletal morphology, and neuromuscular electrophysiological information. Self-tunning mechanism of muscle groups; carry out three-dimensional reconstruction of musculoskeletal structure and kinematics simulation, predict the mechanical mechanism of human joints, identify muscle groups and components that play a key role in natural gait, and quantify the mutual cooperation between targeted muscle groups. To study the best collaborative driving method of robot joints in natural gait, optimize the design of the driving position and driving force of the walking robot joints for knee exoskeleton has high bionic human knee joint motion characteristics, and experimental verification. The implementation of this project is expected to provide a theoretical basis for the design of knee joint exoskeleton, and to achieve functional bionic compensation for personalized self-tunning functions, which is instructive for high-efficiency driving of the lower limb wearable device.
