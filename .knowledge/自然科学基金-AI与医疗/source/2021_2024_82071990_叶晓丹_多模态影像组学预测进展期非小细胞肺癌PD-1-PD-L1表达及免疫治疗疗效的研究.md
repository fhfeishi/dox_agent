## 中文摘要

免疫治疗已成为非小细胞肺癌（NSCLC）重要治疗方法，现行疗效评价标准因存在非常规缓解模式而不能实现准确评价。本课题拟基于增强CT、动态增强MRI参数图和体素内不相干运动扩散加权成像参数图（D、D*和f map）经三维多模态影像融合，并以时间编码的神经网络法分割病灶，用影像组学方法提取特征，采用LASSO回归筛选特征参数，分别联合可切除NSCLC的PD-1/PD-L1等生物标记物表达情况和晚期免疫治疗疗效，建立基于上述单/多模态图像影像组学模型，通过比较单/多模态影像组学模型诊断效能，分别筛选出用于预测PD-1/PD-L1表达和免疫治疗疗效的最佳影像组学模型；再结合Logistic回归筛选临床及实验室指标，建立预测晚期肺癌免疫治疗疗效的临床-影像组学模型，建立nomogram，评价其效能，最终实现疗效的高效预测，为临床肿瘤免疫治疗疗效评价提供一种影像学新方法。

## 英文摘要

Immunotherapies comprise of a class of cancer therapies that are increasingly used for treatment of several cancer entities. Immune checkpoint inhibitor anti-programmed cell death protein (PD)-1 or anti-programmed cell death ligand 1 (PD-L1) monotherapy is one of the most widespread class of immunotherapies for non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Immune checkpoint inhibitors have demonstrated unique response patterns that are not adequately captured by traditional response criteria. In this study, we aim to develop innovative multimodality radiomics-based predictors of response to immunotherapy to improve treatment outcomes. First, contrast enhanced CT, dynamic enhanced MRI and intravoxel incoherent motion diffusion weighted imaging (IVIM-DWI) examination will be performed on patients with surgically resected peripheral NSCLC. Immunohistochemical (IHC) examination with PD-1/PD-L1 antibodies to assess for biomarker such as PD-1/PD-L1 expression on either tumor cells or tumor-infiltrating immune cells on surgical resection specimens. The radiomics approach include multi-modality image fusion, image segmentation, quantitative feature extraction, feature selection and classification. Ten radiomic feature sets were extracted from contrast-enhanced CT; Ktrans, Kep ,Ve calculated from DCE-MRI; D maps, D* maps and f maps calculated from IVIM-DWI; as well as multi-modality containing the above. Ten radiomics models with radiomics features will be constructed by the least absolute shrinkage and selection operator algorithm to predict PD-1/PD-L1 expression on tumor tissue. The relative strength and predictive accuracy of these ten radiomics models will be compared and the most appropriate radiomics model will be selected. Second, multimodality images including contrast-enhanced CT; Ktrans, Kep and Ve calculated from DCE-MRI; D maps, D* maps and f maps calculated from IVIM-DWI will be collected from advanced NSCLC patients before receiving anti-PD-1/PD-L1 monotherapy. Ten radiomics models with radiomics features will be extracted from CT images; Ktrans, Kep and Ve calculated from DCE-MRI; D maps, D* maps, f maps; as well as multi-modality image fusion containing the above sequences and constructed by the least absolute shrinkage and selection operator algorithm to predict the immunotherapy effect. Baseline multi-modality imaging data segmentations and their fusion diagrams are temporally encoded by the artificial intelligence (AI) framework named NoFoNet, which outperforms direct prediction by a U-Net in terms of visual quality so as to predict progress time of the tumor. The relative strength and predictive accuracy of mono and multi-modality image fusion will be compared and the most appropriate model will be selected to construct the clinical-radiomics model with significant clinical and laboratory variables selected by the stepwise logistic regression. The nomogram will be developed based on the clinical-radiomics model. Decision-curve analysis will be applied to assess the clinical usefulness.

## 结题摘要

本研究聚焦非小细胞肺癌（non-small cell lung cancer, NSCLC）异质性导致的生物标记物PD-1/PD-L1表达及其治疗后演进的影像与临床评估难题，在前期NSCLC生物学标记物及疗效评价研究方法学基础上，通过多维度探索，包括基线增强CT、新辅助治疗前后增量影像机器学习模型及MRI功能成像定量参数的评价效能，构建了一种基于多任务学习的可视化肿瘤演进预测模型。该模型在预测肿瘤进展与否的分类效能上表现出色，AUC值高达0.89。此外，基于随访配准的深度学习联合影像组学的双增量免疫治疗疗效评价模型在外部中心队列验证中AUC达到0.85，进一步提升了疗效评价的可视化稳定性。同时，双增量模型在高危成分STAS的跨中心验证中表现出良好的泛化性能，两个外部队列的AUC值分别为0.844和0.840，准确率为80.6%和81.1%，敏感度为75.0%和94.1%，特异性为83.3%和75.0%。.在MRI多参数研究中，功能MRI成像IVIM的定量参数ADC和D的风险降低幅度为56.2%至82.7%，表明ADC和D参数在预测NSCLC对抗肿瘤治疗反应方面均为无进展生存期（PFS）的独立预测因子，可作为早期疗效评价的重要辅助指标。此外，本研究开发了一种基于优化纳米颗粒的激光解吸/电离质谱平台的多重检测方法，结合血清代谢指纹（SMFs）、蛋白质肿瘤标志物和图像特征，构建了双模态（MP-NN）和三模态（MPI-RF）深度学习模型，显著提升了肺腺癌早期检测和肺结节分类的性能（p < 0.001）。同时，提出了一种基于环形偶极子干涉增强电共振Q因数的策略，设计了一种由电偶极子和环形偶极子谐振器组成的超表面，并将其应用于高灵敏度太赫兹生物传感器，实现了肺癌细胞的无标记快速检测和介电常数精确估算，其折射率灵敏度达485.3 GHz/RIU，且在斜入射角下表现出优异的角度稳定性，为肿瘤细胞检测和临床诊断提供了新方法。.综上所述，本研究通过多尺度、多视角、多任务学习及深度学习等技术，显著提升了NSCLC演进预测和疗效评估的准确性和适用性，为计算机辅助诊断和个性化治疗提供了有力的理论支持与实践基础。
