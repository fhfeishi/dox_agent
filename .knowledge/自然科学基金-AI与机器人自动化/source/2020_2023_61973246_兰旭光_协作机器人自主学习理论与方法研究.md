## 中文摘要

围绕机器人自主作业和自主学习的挑战，本课题重点研究协作场景中基于深度强化学习的机器人决策理论和算法，对协作中的任务理解、多任务学习、类人动作的学习等难点和要素进行解析并建模，将其与深度强化学习算法融合，实现在复杂任务中机器人自主学习与作业。研究内容主要包括高探索效率的策略优化；基于概念学习的复杂场景下的任务建模，结合人类专家的先验知识，使机器人能够在不同场景下自主地理解；基于迁移学习的模拟-真实模型迁移，将在模拟环境中充分预训练模型迁移到真实的机器人上；基于模仿学习的机器人类人动作学习，在专家数据的指导下基于少量样本训练机器人学习类人的运动策略，用于机器人的运动规划和不同任务下作业的学习；基于Baxter机器人设计并搭建自主学习任务的系统实验平台，验证所提出算法的有效性。本课题可为形成具有我国自主知识产权的机器人的核心算法和关键技术提供有力支撑，具有重要的应用价值。

## 英文摘要

Focusing on the challenges of robotic autonomous manipulation and learning, this project focuses on robot decision theory and algorithms based on deep reinforcement learning in collaborative scenarios, and models task understanding, multitasking learning, and human-like action learning in collaboration, and so on. Then it is integrated to realize robot autonomous learning and manipulation in complex tasks. It mainly includes policy optimization with high-efficiency exploration; task modeling in complex scenarios based on concept learning, combined with prior knowledge of human experts to enable robots to autonomously understand the task in different scenarios; simulation-reality transferring model based on transfer learning, fully pre-trained models in simulation environments transferred to real robots; human-like motion learning based on imitation learning, training robots to learn human-like motion strategies based on a small number of samples for robotic motion planning with different tasks. Finally we will design a system experiment platform based on robot Baxter to verify the effectiveness of the proposed algorithms. This project can provide strong support for the formation of core algorithms and key technologies of robots with independent intellectual property rights in China, and has important value of applications.
