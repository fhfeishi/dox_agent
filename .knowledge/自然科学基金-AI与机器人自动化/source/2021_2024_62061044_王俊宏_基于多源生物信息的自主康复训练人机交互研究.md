## 中文摘要

随着人口老龄化，运动障碍患者急剧增加。机器人辅助患者自主康复训练是最有效的康复手段。本项目从人体运动和康复机理出发，采集人体sEMG、加速度信号、阵列触觉信号，经滤波、消噪、放大等预处理后，提取各路信号的ADL和关节运动特征，进行多信号特征选择、特征融合，结合改进的Hill肌肉模型创新提出一种关节连续运动估计模型，通过闭环预测算法对人体关节连续运动进行估计，解码人体ADL和多关节的连续运动角度、速度、加速度等信息，将解码结果转化为相应的运动控制指令，控制康复机器人执行人体同步运动，完全根据患者的运动意图进行康复训练。提出融合多生物信号与视觉虚拟现实、触觉反馈的模糊神经网络控制器，将自主康复训练机器人与人体关节两个原本相互独立的控制系统通过人体多生物运动信息解码和视觉触觉反馈重新构建起基于模糊神经网络控制器的人体闭环控制系统，实现人体多关节-康复机器人的人机交互，建立自主康复系统。

## 英文摘要

With the increasing of the elderly population, patients with dyskinesia have increased dramatically. Robot-assisted active rehabilitation treatment is the most effective means of rehabilitation. This project present a limb self-rehabilitation training human-robot Interaction based on multi-source biological signals. The sEMG, acceleration and array tactile signals in lower limb are captured to be filtered, de-noised and amplified. Then the features of ADL and joint movements are extracted，selected and fused. A state-space model is proposed to estimate simultaneous and continuous joint movements. The model combines the forward dynamics with Hill-based muscle model (HMM), making the extended model capable of estimating joint motion directly. The decoding result is converted to the instruction that control the rehabilitation robot to do the same motion. The fuzzy neural network controller is proposed based on the fusion of multi-biometric signal and the feedback of visual virtual reality and tactile. The rehabilitation robot and human limb is rebuilt to a closed loop control system based on fuzzy neural network controller through biological signal and the visual, and tactile feedback. Eventually, we realize the human-machine interaction between the human joint and rehabilitation robot, and establish an autonomous rehabilitation system.
