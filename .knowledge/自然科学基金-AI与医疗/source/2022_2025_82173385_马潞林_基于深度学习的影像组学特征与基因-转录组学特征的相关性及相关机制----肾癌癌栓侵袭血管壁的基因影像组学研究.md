## 中文摘要

肾细胞癌的分子特征对其生物学行为及药物选择至关重要，但常难以获取用于测序的组织。CT/MRI提供的影像学数据虽易获取，但人眼仅可感知其中极少数信息，大部分信息未被利用且难以在分子水平解释。我们的科学假设为：通过基于深度学习的算法可能找到影像组学特征与肿瘤分子特征的内在联系，从而达到通过影像学检查提示肿瘤分子特征的目的。我们预实验发现：①血管侵袭性癌栓（DITT）和非侵袭性癌栓（NITT）之间有明显的基因/转录组差异；②二者同时存在明显的影像组学差异。从算法角度而言，DITT对NITT这一“是或否”的逻辑关系，是探索基因组与影像组学关联的良好模型。我们拟①通过AI算法探索在癌栓是否侵袭血管壁的问题上，分子特征与影像组学特征的内在联系；②通过动物成瘤影像学实验，探索这种相关性的内在物质基础。本研究在理论层面为关联肾癌影像组学与基因组学的原创探索，在应用方面将影像临床诊断的功能向分子水平延伸。

## 英文摘要

The molecular characteristics of renal cell carcinoma are critical to tumoral biological behavior and therapeutic options, but it is often difficult to obtain tumoral specimens for sequencing through invasive approach. Although the imaging data provided by CT/MRI are easy to obtain, only a few fractions of them can be transferred to visible signals for imaging diagnosis, and the rest of them have to be ignored and difficult to interpret at molecular level. Our scientific hypothesis is that it is possible to setup the inner correlations between radiomic features and molecular features through deep learning algorithms, so as to achieve the purpose of elaborate tumor molecular features through imaging approach. Our preliminary studies demonstrated that: ① there are remarkable genomic/transcriptomic differences between deep venous invasive tumor thrombus (DITT) and non-invasive tumor thrombus (NITT); ② there are obvious radiomic differences between the two cohorts. From an algorithmic point of view, the "yes or no" logical relationship between DITT and NITT provides an ingenious model for exploring the association between genomics/transcriptomics and radiomics. We plan to ① apply deep learning algorithms to explore the internal relationship between molecular features and radiomic features on the issue of whether the tumor thrombus invades the blood vessel wall; ② delineate the intrinsic nature of this correlation through animal tumor imaging experiments. At the theoretical level, this research is an original exploration of the association between renal cancer radiomics and genomics; moreover, it may extend the application of imaging-based diagnosis to the molecular level, clinically.

## 结题摘要

肾细胞癌的分子特征对其生物学行为及药物选择至关重要，但常难以获取用于测序的组织。CT/MRI提供的影像学数据虽易获取，但人眼仅可感知其中极少数信息，大部分信息未被利用且难以在分子水平解释。在本项目中，共采集202例癌栓患者的CT影像，进行影像组学研究，采集相对应的73例癌栓标本进行了转录组学测序，经过特征筛选和相关性分析后获得13个影像特征和6个基因特征与癌栓侵袭有关，构建了癌栓侵袭血管壁的基因影像联合预测模型，在验证集中AUC高达0.922，在测试集中AUC也可达到0.838，证明模型可有效预测癌栓侵犯血管壁
