## 中文摘要

DLBCL患者的难治/复发问题，目前仍是困扰临床的一大难题。因此，寻求更佳的新作用靶点，进一步及时有效评估患者预后，顺应临床需求。在我们前期研究提示TCF4与DLBCL患者OS和PFS预后指标相关，NF-kappa B可能参与TCF4调控及借助autoencoder深度学习方法，建立DLBCL预后模型的基础上，拟从临床样本及细胞系检测、动物模型及体外实验三个角度，从现象观察及机制探索两个方面探究TCF4在DLBCL中的作用机制，并借助深度学习理论技术等方法，探究TCF4能否作为DLBCL患者预后的重要指标。该研究如获成功，有望为DLBCL治疗提供新的作用靶点及更准确评估DLBCL预后，提供新思路和理论依据。

## 英文摘要

The refractory/relapse problem of diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) patients is still a major problem that plaques the clinic. Therefore, it is necessary to seek better new targets, further timely and effectively evaluate the prognosis of patients, which comply with the clinical needs. Based on our previous studies, transcription factor 4 (TCF4) was related to the prognostic indicators of overall survival and progression-free survival in DLBCL patients, NF-kappa B might be involved in the regulation of TCF4 and the utilization of autoencoder deep learning methods to establish a prognostic model of DLBCL. Thus, we intend to explore the pathogenesis of TCF4 in the occurrence and development of DLBCL from three angles: the testing of clinical samples and cell lines, in vivo and in vitro experiments and two aspects: phenomenon and mechanism. In addition, with the help of deep learning and other methods, we investigate whether TCF4 could be considered as an important indicator of the prognosis of patients with DLBCL. If successful, it might provide new targets for the treatment of DLBCL, more acutely assess the prognosis of DLBCL as well as new ideas and theoretical basis.
