## 中文摘要

甲状腺癌是内分泌系统和头颈部最常见的恶性肿瘤，其中甲状腺乳头状癌是临床最常见的分型，发病率占据甲状腺癌的85％以上，预后较差。高频超声是甲状腺结节良恶性筛查的首选手段，其筛查结果常受操作者人工判读的主客观因素影响，误诊漏诊常见，已不能满足临床诊断的需求。随着人工智能技术的快速发展，如何将甲状腺癌的临床诊疗与其相结合，为甲状腺良恶性结节鉴别建立最佳的疾病诊断模型，从而弥补超声检查的各种不足是项目需要解决的关键科学问题。因此，项目以前期收集的甲状腺乳头状癌患者的结节超声图像为基础，综合发挥交叉学科的优势，联合计算机建模和临床超声影像学技术，在建立甲状腺乳头状癌超声图像预测模型的基础上，设计并建立甲状腺乳头状癌智能检测系统和智能诊断共享平台，为全面提升甲状腺癌的临床筛查效率及推动重要医疗信息共享奠定基础，同时也为人工智能与其他肿瘤疾病诊疗技术的结合开辟了新的研究路径。

## 英文摘要

Thyroid cancer is the most common malignant tumor of the head, neck and endocrine system. Papillary thyroid carcinoma is the most common clinical type, the incidence accounting for more than 85% of thyroid cancer, and the prognosis is poor. High-frequency ultrasound is the preferred method for benign and malignant screening of thyroid nodules. The screening results are often affected by subjective and objective factors of operator's manual interpretation, misdiagnosis is common, which cannot meet the needs of clinical diagnosis. With the rapid development of artificial intelligence technology, the key scientific problem to be solved in this project is how to combine the clinical diagnosis and treatment of thyroid cancer with it, establish the best disease diagnosis model for differentiating benign and malignant thyroid nodules, so as to make up for various deficiencies of ultrasound examination. Consequently, this project is based on previously collected ultrasound images of nodules in patients with papillary thyroid carcinoma, give full play to the advantages of interdisciplinary, combined computer modeling and clinical ultrasound imaging techniques. Based on the establishment of an ultrasound image prediction model for papillary thyroid carcinoma, an intelligent detection system and intelligent diagnosis sharing platform for papillary thyroid carcinoma are designed and established. It lays a foundation for comprehensively improving the clinical screening efficiency of thyroid cancer and promoting the sharing of important medical information, and also opens up a new research path for the combination of artificial intelligence and other tumor disease diagnosis and treatment technology.
