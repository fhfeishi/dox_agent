## 中文摘要

免疫治疗已成进展期肝细胞癌（HCC）综合治疗的重要手段，但仍存在耐药率高的显著问题。多参数功能MRI 能无创反映HCC的生物学行为及免疫微环境；而影像组学则能进一步量化肿瘤的微观表型，并通过人工智能技术构建精准的预测模型。因此，融合上述技术，有望为全面、个体化的HCC免疫治疗耐药提供有力的预测手段。肿瘤免疫微环境是介导HCC免疫治疗耐药的核心途径；申请人前期发现，基于T1 mapping、DWI、MR灌注成像等功能MRI序列的定性及定量分析能反映HCC的免疫微环境，为免疫治疗耐药预测提供重要信息；而病理学分析则能进一步为上述影像表现的生物学机制提供关键的理论依据。因此，本项目拟融合多参数功能 MRI、影像组学及人工智能技术，构建能反映肿瘤免疫微环境、预测HCC免疫治疗耐药的无创风险分层体系，并基于病理学分析，探索 HCC免疫治疗“病理-影像-临床” 间的核心关联机制，辅助HCC的精准治疗。

## 英文摘要

Immunotherapy has become one of the most pivotal treatment options for patients with advanced stage hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), but its clinical utility is hampered by substantial treatment resistance. Multiparametric functional MRI could characterize HCC biological behaviors  and immune microenviroment at structural, functional, and metabolic levels. On this basis, radiomics allows further quantitative profiling of tumor micro-phenotypes and construction of accurate predictive models via artificial intelligence techniques. Therefore, integration of the above techniques shows promise in providing effective noninvasive approaches for comprehensive and individualized immunotherapy resistance prediction. The immune microenviroment plays a core role in mediating immunotherapy resistance. Our previous work demonstrated that qualitative and quantitative analyses of multiparametric functional MRI (e.g., T1 mapping, DWI, MR perfusion imaging, etc.) could help profile the entire tumor immune microenviroment, and offer important information regarding immunotherapy resistance. Pathological analysis may further reveal the key biological underpinnings of the above imaging features. Therefore, the purpose of this study is to integrate multiparametric MRI, radiomics, and artificial intelligence techniques to construct a noninvasive risk stratification system for HCC immune microenvironment profiling and immunotherapy resistance prediction. Furthermore, we also aim to explore the key “pathology-imaging-clinical” correlations in HCC immunotherapy with pathological analysis, and eventually to enable a precision medicine approach in HCC cancer care.

## 结题摘要

肝细胞癌（HCC）是我国发病率第四位、死亡率第二位的恶性肿瘤。免疫治疗是HCC综合治疗的重要手段，但受限于个体间疗效差异大、治疗有效率较低。融合多参数MRI、影像组学及人工智能技术，有望为HCC免疫治疗疗效的精准预测提供有力的无创手段。本项目建立了HCC免疫治疗多中心队列，提出了基于多参数MRI的无创风险分层体系、探索了“病理-影像-临床”核心关联机制。本项目共标注SCI论文22篇（4项原始研究发表于影像学顶刊Radiology、1项原始研究发表于外科学顶刊Int J Surg，1项原始研究获 2023 年European Radiology ESGAR Silver Award）；项目负责人授权一项国家发明专利、参编两项HCC指南共识。本项目较为系统地阐释了多参数MRI在评估HCC侵袭性、辅助HCC预后评价及精准治疗中的巨大潜能和重要作用，研究成果有望推动影像引导的HCC精准治疗，改善患者整体生存。
