# 多模态多流形多深度神经网络的协同-对抗建模机制及其在阿尔茨海默病认知与辅助诊断中的应用研究

## 中文摘要

阿尔茨海默病（AD）具有病理复杂、长潜伏期等特点，在临床诊断中存在病情发展变化规律不清楚、早期诊断不及时等问题。采用流形学习、深度学习分析多模态脑影像及相关异构数据,研究多模态数据的多流形多深度神经网络的协同-对抗建模与优化算法,为AD认知和辅助诊断提供解决方案。(1)针对AD演变轨迹问题，通过测地线的指数簇表示、积流形和混合效应模型,研究多模态多流形轨迹模型及估计算法；(2)针对脑影像缺失问题,在共享内在流形约束下构建与训练双向多生成多对抗网络,在保持病理不变性下研究MRI↔PET的双向生成以及不同参数下的MRI图像生成模型；(3)针对AD脑影像智能理解问题,通过图像深度特征提取模型与语言生成模型来设计端到端的诊断报告自动生成方法，实现脑影像诊断报告的自动生成；(4)针对AD早期诊断问题，借鉴多模态数据采样自多内在流形的假设，在多图正则化下研究多深度神经网络协同的疾病分型与症状评估方法。

## 英文摘要

Alzheimer's Disease (AD) has the characteristics of complex pathology and long incubation period, so there exists some problems in clinical diagnosis, such as unclear law of disease development, untimely early diagnosis, and so on. By using manifold learning and deep learning to analyze multimodal brain images and AD’s related heterogeneous data, the aim is to research the collaborative-adversarial modeling mechanism of multi-manifold multiple deep neural networks and their optimization algorithms for multi-modal AD data, which provides the solutions for AD cognition and aided diagnosis. (1) Aiming at the problem of AD evolution trajectory, the proposal will research the multimodal multi-manifold trajectory model and the trajectory estimation algorithm through the exponential family representation of geodesics, product manifold and mixed effect model; (2) Aiming at the absence of brain images, the bi-directional multi-generative multi-adversarial neural networks will be constructed and trained under the constraint of sharing intrinsic manifold, so, through preserving the pathological invariance, the bidirectional generation model of MRI↔PET and the generation model of MRI with different parameters will be researched; (3) Aiming at the intelligent understanding of AD’s brain images, based on the image deep feature extraction model and language generation model, an end-to-end medical diagnostic report auto-generation method will be designed to realize the automatic generation of brain image diagnostic report; (4) Aiming at the early diagnosis of AD, referring the hypothesis of multi-modal data sampling from multiple intrinsic manifolds, the disease classification and symptom evaluation will be researched by using the fusion of multiple deep neural networks under the multi-graph regularization.

## 结题摘要

本项目研究了多模态多流形多深度神经网络的协同建模机制，利用多深度神经网络协同建模、生成对抗学习、连续学习、流形正则化、知识驱动等技术，构建了知识驱动的多图卷积神经网络模型、多流形多模态哈希检索模型、非完整图像的多生成对抗网络哈希检索模型、双向哈希码-图像转换模型、在线深度跨模态哈希检索模型、知识与数据联合驱动的目标检测模型等多深度神经网络协同模型，开展了图像检索、医学图像合成、医学图像恢复、医学图像智能理解、医学图像2D/3D分割、脑网络分析等多维度研究，实现了以阿尔茨海默病为代表的多种疾病辅助诊断与预测。在《Medical Image Analysis》、《IEEE Transactions on Image Process》、《Pattern Recognition》、《Neural Networks》、《Knowledge-Based Systems》、《Information Science》、《IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics》、《IEEE Transactions on Multimedia》、《ACM Transactions on Multimedia Computing, Communications, and Applications》、《中国科学：信息科学》、《电子与信息学报》、《南京大学学报(自然科学)》等高水平学术刊物上发表学术论文32篇（其中SCI期刊论文27篇），受理/授权国家发明专利10项，登记软件著作权3件。
